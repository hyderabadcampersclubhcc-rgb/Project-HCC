# HCC Seasonal Calendar

**Rule:** prepare 6–10 weeks before a peak, and publish and index 4–6 weeks before it. Seasons below are general travel knowledge and trend observations (**inference**), not measured search data. Replace them with Search Console seasonality once 12 months of data exist.

**Holiday dates for 2027** come from published long-weekend lists (Wego, CalendarLabs, Offistrip and others, checked 8 Oct 2026). **Lunar-calendar festivals can shift.** Re-verify against the official Government of India 2027 holiday list and the Telangana state list before publishing anything.

## Season map (Hyderabad origin)

| Period | Hyderabad context (inference) | Push | Hold back |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Oct–Nov** | Post-monsoon, festive season (Dussehra, Diwali) | Coorg, Gokarna, Hampi, Araku (pre-Vanajangi), camping | Monsoon treks |
| **Dec–Jan** | Winter, Christmas and New Year, Republic Day | Araku/Vanajangi, Ooty, Gokarna, Goa, Pondicherry, camping (peak) | — |
| **Feb–Mar** | Pleasant; Maha Shivratri; Holi; Good Friday | Isha (Shivratri), Hampi, Coorg, Gokarna, camping | — |
| **Apr–Jun** | Hyderabad summer heat; school holidays | Ooty, Coorg, Munnar, Wayanad, Kashmir, Manali (hill stations) | Coastal and Hampi (heat), camping (heat) |
| **Jun–Sep** | Monsoon | Sahyadri treks, Lonavala & Matheran, Dudhsagar, Wayanad/Coorg (greenery) | Gokarna/beach promotion (rough sea; verify per trip) |

## Monthly checklist

At the start of each month:
1. Check what peaks in 6–10 weeks (table above), and draft or refresh those items (`content-calendar.md`).
2. Check that departures exist in trip data for every trip being promoted.
3. Check that seasonal guides still show correct dates. Live components update automatically, but written dates in the text don't.
4. After the season ends, record what Search Console shows (impressions by month) so next year's plan uses real data.
