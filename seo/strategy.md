# HCC SEO Strategy (permanent)

**Owner:** Hyderabad Campers Club (HCC) · **Created:** 8 Oct 2026 · **Review:** monthly (see `monthly-report-template.md`)

This is the standing SEO strategy for hyderabadcampersclubhcc.in. Every SEO recommendation should be traceable to this file, to `keyword-universe.json`, or (once connected) to Google Search Console data.

---

## 1. What HCC is trying to win

**Objective:** be the obvious, trusted answer when someone in **Hyderabad** (and, once operationally complete, **Bangalore**) searches for a **group trip, weekend getaway or camping night** to a destination HCC actually runs, and turn that visit into a WhatsApp enquiry or booking.

We are **not** trying to rank for:
- generic destination searches ("Ooty", "Goa", "Kerala", "India tourism"), which are dominated by Wikipedia, government tourism sites, OTAs and big publishers;
- destinations or trip types HCC doesn't sell;
- international travel, even though it dominates India's trend lists (Google Year in Search 2025: Maha Kumbh, Philippines, Georgia, Mauritius, Kashmir, Phu Quoc, Phuket, Maldives, Somnath, Pondicherry). *Source: Forbes India / India TV, Dec 2025.*

## 2. Audiences

1. **Working professionals in Hyderabad (core):** want to get away Thursday or Friday night and be back Monday or Tuesday morning, on a budget, in a group. Main triggers: long weekends, the winter season, friends' plans falling through.
2. **Solo travellers:** want a safe, organised group. They search "solo trip", "group trips for solo travellers".
3. **Friend groups (4–10 people):** compare destinations and prices, and want a custom or private version.
4. **Couples:** served by the couples trips (Ooty, Gokarna) and Kerala honeymoon.
5. **Bangalore professionals:** a secondary market, gated until Bangalore operations are complete (see §10).
6. **Corporate and college groups:** future. No real offer data yet, so no SEO push.

## 5. Commercial keyword strategy

- **One page per intent.**
  - Trip pages own "X trip from Hyderabad" / "X package from Hyderabad" (booking intent).
  - Destination guides own "X travel guide / best time / how to reach" (informational, with links to trips).
  - City hubs own "weekend trips from Hyderabad" and "group trips from Hyderabad".
  - The camping hub owns "camping near Hyderabad".
- **Price is a commercial signal.** Searches with "cost", "price", "budget" or "under ₹X" are high intent. Answer them on the trip page first: a clear price block and what's included. Write a separate guide only when the intent differs, e.g. a cost breakdown comparing several trips.
- **Don't duplicate trips per keyword variant.** "Ooty package from Hyderabad", "Ooty tour from Hyderabad" and "Ooty trip from Hyderabad" are the same intent and belong on one page.

## 6. Informational strategy

Write informational content only when it:
1. answers a real planning question HCC can answer from first-hand trip data (route, train, timings, cost, what's included, season),
2. has a different intent from the trip page and destination guide,
3. links naturally to a bookable HCC trip.

Formats that fit HCC: route/transport guides, comparisons, cost and inclusion guides, seasonal and long-weekend guides, solo/couple/group guides, packing and camping guides.

## 7. Seasonal strategy

See `seasonal-calendar.md`. Rule: **prepare 6–10 weeks before the peak; publish and index 4–6 weeks before.** HCC's dated Thursday/Friday batches are the asset: seasonal content must point to real dates, never invented ones.

## 11. AI search / answer engines

Goal: make HCC's **real facts** easy to extract, not to "game" AI. See the "AI-search layer" section in `topic-clusters.md`.
- Keep each trip's facts consistent everywhere: departure days and times, return, price, transport, stay, inclusions, difficulty. The trip data is the single source of truth.
- Prefer clear tables and short factual sections. Use Included / Not included tables (one price per person, never upgrade prices).
- Keep Organization identity consistent: name, logo, address, phone, sameAs links (Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps).
- Google AI Overviews reduce clicks on informational travel queries. Industry studies report steep CTR drops on queries that show an Overview (e.g. a Seer Interactive study cited by TravelTalk, late 2025). *These are global or US figures; India-specific data wasn't found.* So **weight effort toward booking-intent pages**, and treat informational content as support for those pages, not as a traffic goal in itself.

## 12. Internal linking

Hub → destination guide → trip page, with supporting guides linking into trips. See `internal-linking.md`. Every new page gets inbound links from at least 2 relevant existing pages before it's indexed.

## 13. Authority

Content alone won't beat established operators. Reviews, Google Business Profile, real photos and genuine mentions are the limiting factors. See `authority-plan.md`.

## 14. YouTube / video

Short, real trip videos (Reels/Shorts) per Tier 1 destination, linked to the trip page. Never present AI imagery as real trips. See `authority-plan.md` §6.

## 15. Content quality rules (hard rules)

1. **Trip data is the source of truth** (`src/content/trips/`, `src/content/departures/`). If an article disagrees with the trip data, the article is wrong.
2. **Never invent:** prices, dates, schedules, departure/return times, transport, hotels, room types, inclusions, exclusions, reviews, ratings, traveller numbers, popularity or booking claims, availability, search volumes.
3. **If data is missing, say it's missing.** Don't guess; log it as a data gap.
4. Prices in articles come from live components (`TripTable`, `TripCallout`) wherever possible, so they can't go stale.
5. No "best", "most popular" or "No. 1" claims without evidence.
6. AI-generated images are labelled "AI-generated illustration" and are never presented as HCC or customer photos.
7. Original wording only. Competitors are used for strategy, never copied.
8. **One customer-facing price per person.** Never show separate prices for train class (sleeper/3AC), sleeper bus, room type or sharing (quad/triple/double/twin), or any other transport or stay variation, unless the owner approves a trip-specific exception. Transport and stay may be described as facts. If HCC can arrange an alternative when asked, describe it only as "May be possible on request — ask HCC on WhatsApp", with no price. Never create SEO content that treats an upgrade price as a product (approved 8 Oct 2026).

## 16. Anti-duplicate rules

- Before proposing a page, check `keyword-universe.json` → `hccPage`. If a page already owns the intent, **improve it**.
- No city × destination pages that duplicate trip pages. No "weekend getaways" page that duplicates the hub. No new pages for keyword variants.
- No `/camping-near-bangalore/` until real Bangalore camps exist.
- Watch cannibalisation pairs, e.g. the Ooty guide vs the Ooty trip page, or the "Under ₹8,000" article vs the Hyderabad hub. Use Search Console to check which page Google picks.

## 17. Publishing rules (human approval required)

Automation may research, analyse, update reports and draft. **The owner approves before:** publishing an article, making any page indexable, changing important page copy, changing prices, dates, schedules, transport, inclusions or accommodation, creating new indexable pages, or deleting or redirecting pages.

Workflow: **RESEARCH → ANALYSE → PRIORITISE → RECOMMEND → DRAFT (on approval) → OWNER APPROVES → PUBLISH.**
