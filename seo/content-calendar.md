# HCC Content Calendar (Oct 2026 → Sep 2027)

**Rules:**
- Improve existing pages before creating new ones.
- Every item needs owner approval before it's published or made indexable.
- Prices and dates always come from trip data.
- Capacity assumption: **1–2 content items per month**, plus page improvements. This is a small team, so the plan favours quality over volume.

Status values:
- `proposed`: waiting for owner approval
- `approved`
- `drafted`
- `published`
- `blocked (reason)`

- Any page created only to target AI search.
