The week between Christmas and New Year is one of the easiest times to take a trip: many offices are quieter, and a couple of days' leave stretches into a long break. It's also when seats go fastest. This guide lists every HCC group departure from Hyderabad over Christmas 2026 and New Year 2027, with return dates, so you can plan your leave around them.

**Status:** the departures below are confirmed in HCC's schedule as of 27 October 2026. Seats depend on availability; the trip page shows the live status for each date.

## Choosing a festive trip

### Where will you be on New Year's Eve?

All the 31 December departures leave Hyderabad that day, so you'll ring in the New Year on the overnight train or on arrival, depending on the trip and departure time. If being somewhere specific at midnight matters to you, ask HCC on WhatsApp before booking. No special New Year events have been announced; the trips follow their normal itineraries.

### Beach, hills or forest?

- **Beach and river:** Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar. Late December is usually the busiest time on the Karnataka coast; see [the best time to visit Gokarna and Dandeli](/travel-guides/best-time-to-visit-gokarna-and-dandeli/).
- **Cool hills:** Ooty & Coonoor, Coorg & Chikmagalur, Wayanad. Winter nights are cold in the hills, so pack warm layers.
- **Hills and coast together:** Araku Valley with Vizag.
- **Hills and a lakeside camp:** Lonavala & Matheran.

### How much leave do you need?

- **24 December departures** returning on 27 December need little or no leave if 25 December is a holiday for you.
- **25 December departures** return on 28 or 29 December.
- **31 December departures** return on 3 or 4 January 2027.

Check your own holiday calendar; office holidays vary.

## Booking tips for the festive season

1. **Book early.** Festive departures fill faster than regular weekends, and train berths are booked with the group.
2. **Check the price for your date** on the trip page when you select it.
3. **Read the inclusions.** Most trips don't include all meals; restaurants can be busier over the holidays.
4. **Know the policy.** {{advance-summary}} See the [cancellation policy](/cancellation-policy/).
5. **Pack for winter.** Hills are cold at night in late December. See the [packing checklist](/travel-guides/what-to-pack-for-a-camping-trip/).

## Prefer one night close to home?

If a long trip isn't possible, the [Ananthagiri Hills camps](/camping-near-hyderabad/) are about 80 km from Hyderabad. Check availability for your date on WhatsApp.
