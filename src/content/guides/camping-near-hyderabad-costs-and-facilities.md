"Camping" covers a lot, from a tent in the wild to a resort lawn with a pool. Near Hyderabad, what you'll mostly find are **organised campsites**: tents set up for you, meals cooked on site, shared washrooms and planned activities. This guide explains what camps near Hyderabad cost, what the price covers, and the facility questions worth asking before you pay. For the list of HCC campsites with photos and booking, see [camping near Hyderabad](/camping-near-hyderabad/).

## Where you can camp

### Ananthagiri Hills (near Vikarabad)

About 80 km from Hyderabad, roughly 2 to 2.5 hours by road. HCC works with four campsites here, each with a different feel: a budget camp with a pool and a Saturday DJ night, an activity camp with a rope course, lakeside tents at Sarpanpally Lake, and a premium camp with a sunrise trek. You reach the campsite yourself. See the [Ananthagiri Hills camping guide](/travel-guides/ananthagiri-hills-camping-guide/).

### Gandikota (Andhra Pradesh)

A canyon on the Pennar river, much further away. HCC's Gandikota trip includes a vehicle from Hyderabad, so it's a full two days. See the [Gandikota camping guide](/travel-guides/gandikota-camping-guide/).

## Facilities: what to expect

### Tents

Tents are pitched and ready when you arrive. Expect bedding or ground bedding depending on the camp, not mattresses and furniture. If you're booking for an odd number of people, ask on WhatsApp how tents will be allotted.

### Washrooms

The Ananthagiri campsites have **common washrooms**, not attached to tents. Carry slippers, a small torch and your own toiletries and towel.

### Food

Meals are buffet or live-kitchen style, at set times. The budget camp's buffet includes bagara rice, chicken curry, veg curry and sambar; breakfast is items such as puri, upma, idli or bonda. Ask on WhatsApp about specific dietary needs.

### Electricity and charging

Don't count on a charging point in your tent. Carry a charged power bank.

### Parking

At the budget camp, parking is free, away from the tents, at the owner's risk.

## Costs outside the price

- **Getting there** (Ananthagiri): fuel, or a train to Vikarabad plus an auto or cab for the last stretch
- **Extras at the camp:** some camps have paid activities; ask before you go
- **Snacks and drinks** beyond the included meals
- **Nearby sightseeing** on your own, such as Kotpalli Lake near Ananthagiri

4. **What safety arrangements are in place** (first aid, staff on site at night, pool supervision)? Contact us on WhatsApp to confirm.
5. **What's the check-in time for my camp?** Fixed at 4 pm for the budget camp; ask for the others.

## Camp rules that affect your plans

- Arrive on time; late arrivals may miss meals or activities.
- Carry ID for every guest. At the budget camp, one Aadhaar copy is shared on WhatsApp when booking.
- No drugs, illegal activity or nuisance; breaking camp rules can mean removal without a refund.
- At the budget camp, alcohol from other states and hookah aren't allowed. At Sarpanpally, outside food, alcohol and hazardous items aren't allowed.
- Damage to tents or equipment is charged at actuals.

## Booking and payment

A ₹500 non-refundable advance per person holds your place at any of these camps. At the budget camp, the remaining amount is paid after you reach the campsite; for the others, the balance deadline is confirmed on WhatsApp. Read the [cancellation policy](/cancellation-policy/) first.

## Which camp for which budget?

- **Lowest price, most included fun:** the budget camp.
- **Adventure activities:** Harivillu.
- **Water views:** Sarpanpally Lake.
- **Pool plus a guided sunrise trek:** Trance Veechika.
- **No car, everything arranged:** Gandikota.
