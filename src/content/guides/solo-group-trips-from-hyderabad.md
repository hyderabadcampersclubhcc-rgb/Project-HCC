Travelling on your own doesn't have to mean planning everything on your own. On a group trip, the train tickets, stays and local transport are arranged, and you share the experience with people who signed up for the same weekend. This guide walks through what actually happens when you book an HCC group trip from Hyderabad alone, step by step. For which trips suit solo travellers, see [solo trips from Hyderabad](/travel-guides/solo-trips-from-hyderabad/).

## Step 1: Choose a trip that runs often

Trips with fixed weekly departures are the easiest to plan around, and they tend to have a steady mix of solo travellers and small groups. Good first options:

{{trip-table: coorg-chikmagalur, araku-vanajangi-vizag, ooty-coonoor, gokarna-dandeli | Weekly group trips from Hyderabad (live prices)}}

If you'd rather try one night first, the [Ananthagiri Hills camps](/camping-near-hyderabad/) are close to the city. Note that you reach the campsite on your own; there's no group transport.

## Step 2: Book and share your details

1. Pick a date on the trip page and tap **Book Your Seat**, or message HCC on WhatsApp.
2. HCC confirms availability and shares payment details.
3. Pay the advance. {{advance-summary}}
4. Share the details the team asks for, such as your name, age and ID, so tickets and stays can be booked.

Ask your questions before you pay: rooming, food, what to carry, return times. It's easier to sort out now than on the platform.

## Step 3: Departure day

On most trips the group meets at the station named on the trip page (Kacheguda, Nampally or Secunderabad) in the evening. The trip captain checks everyone in. Train berths are allotted with the group's tickets, so you may be next to other travellers rather than people you know. That's normal and is usually where introductions start.

## Step 4: A typical day on the trip

Days on a 2-night trip are full:

- **Early start.** Sunrise points and popular viewpoints are visited early. On Coorg & Chikmagalur, the Mullayanagiri sunrise start is at 5 am.
- **Sightseeing by vehicle.** A tempo traveller or AC vehicle moves the group between places.
- **Meals.** Many trips don't include all meals, so the group usually eats together at local restaurants and each person pays for their own food. The trip page lists any included meals.
- **Evenings.** Free time at the stay. Some trips include a bonfire or music night (Araku, for example).

## Step 5: Rooms and tents

You'll share with other travellers on the sharing basis the trip lists, usually 3 or 4 per room or tent. On some trips, such as [Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/), the trip page states that male and female accommodation is arranged separately. If the arrangement matters to you, confirm it on WhatsApp for your departure.

## What it costs a solo traveller

The trip price is the same per person whether you come alone or with friends. Your extra spending is the same as anyone's:

- Meals not included on the trip page
- Entry tickets, jeep rides and activities
- Snacks, water and shopping


## Safety habits that make solo group travel easier

These are sensible habits on any trip, not a substitute for HCC's own arrangements:

- Save the trip captain's number and the HCC WhatsApp number before you leave.
- Share your itinerary and the trip page link with someone at home.
- Keep your ID, phone, cash and cards on you rather than in the main bag.
- Tell the trip captain about any medical condition or dietary need at the start.
- Don't swim, trek off-route or wander alone at night where the trip captain advises against it.

## Is a solo group trip right for you?

It suits you if you'd like company without having to organise it, are comfortable sharing rooms, and are happy with a set itinerary. If you want complete freedom over every stop, travel independently. If you're unsure, start with one night at a camp, then try a weekend trip.
