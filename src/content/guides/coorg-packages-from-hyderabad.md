HCC's Coorg trips come in three versions, and the right one depends mostly on where you start. From Hyderabad the package combines Coorg with Chikmagalur and travels by overnight train. From Bangalore the same route runs by Tempo Traveller, and there's a shorter Coorg-focused trip that starts in Mysore. This page compares them. For the destinations themselves, see the [Coorg and Chikmagalur travel guide](/travel-guides/coorg-and-chikmagalur-travel-guide/).

## Coorg & Chikmagalur from Hyderabad

[View the trip](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-hyderabad/)

**Who it suits:** anyone in Hyderabad who wants both coffee regions in one long weekend.

- **Day 0:** leave Kacheguda at 7 pm.
- **Day 1:** freshen up and breakfast in Mysore, Mysore Palace, Abbey Falls and the Mandalpatti trek; spice and coffee shopping, Raja's Seat if time permits.
- **Day 2:** Dubare elephant camp, Belur temple and water sports at Yagachi dam; night at a coffee plantation.
- **Day 3:** Mullayanagiri at sunrise, breakfast, back to Mysore for the 2:30 pm train.
- **Day 4:** in Hyderabad around 6 am.

## Coorg & Chikmagalur from Bangalore

[View the trip](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-bangalore/)

**Who it suits:** travellers starting in Bangalore, or anyone who'd rather not take the train.

A 6 am pickup on Friday or Saturday, the Golden Temple and Raja's Seat on day 1, Mandalpatti, Abbey Falls, Belur and Yagachi on day 2, and Mullayanagiri and Jhari Falls on day 3 before the drive back.

## Coorg from Mysore

[View the trip](/trips/coorg-from-mysore/)

**Who it suits:** travellers already in Mysore who want a Coorg-only trip at a slower pace. Dates are on request.

## Extra costs on any Coorg trip

- **Food:** not included on any of the three.
- **Jeep rides:** Mandalpatti and the hill roads to Mullayanagiri or Jhari Falls are jeep country.
- **Entry tickets:** waterfalls, palace, elephant camp, temples.
- **Water sports** at Yagachi, if you take them.
