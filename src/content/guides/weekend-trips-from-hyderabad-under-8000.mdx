A weekend away doesn't have to cost a week's salary. These are HCC's trips from Hyderabad that start under ₹8,000 per person, grouped by budget. The prices in the tables are live: they come straight from each trip page.

**What "from" means:** the price is per person and covers what's listed as included.

## Under ₹2,000: one night, close to the city

The cheapest way to get out of Hyderabad is a one-night camp: no leave and no long journey.

<TripTable trips={['narsapur-camping', 'ananthagiri-camping', 'gandikota-camping']} caption="One-night camps (live prices)" />

- **Narsapur** includes a shared tent, tea, dinner and breakfast, a campfire, a movie night and group games. You reach the campsite yourself.
- **Ananthagiri Hills** is a campsite near Vikarabad with a pool and a Saturday music night. You reach it yourself, by car, bike or the train to Vikarabad.
- **Gandikota** includes transport from Hyderabad to the canyon.

All three are on the [camping near Hyderabad](/camping-near-hyderabad/) page.

## Two nights away (from about ₹6,000 to ₹8,000)

These 2-night trips include return train (or bus) tickets, two nights' stay and local transport for the sightseeing.

## Monsoon trips under ₹8,000

Some of HCC's trips under ₹8,000 run only in the monsoon: the Sahyadri treks to [Andharban, Devkund and Lohagad](/trips/andharban-devkund-lohagad-trek-from-hyderabad/) and [Harishchandragad](/trips/harishchandragad-kalu-trek-from-hyderabad/), and [Dudhsagar & Dandeli](/trips/dudhsagar-dandeli-from-hyderabad/). Look for their dates when the rains start.

## Costs to add on top

Plan for these beyond the trip price. Each trip page lists exactly what's included:

- **Food:** see what each trip includes in the table above.
- **Entry tickets, jeep rides and activities:** paid locally. Jeep rides are extra on Coorg & Chikmagalur and Wayanad, and the Ooty toy train ticket is about ₹150.
- **Getting to the campsite:** for Narsapur and Ananthagiri.
