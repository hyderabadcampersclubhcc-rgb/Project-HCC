## Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar: the standard group trip

[View the trip](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-hyderabad/)

**Best for:** friends, solo travellers and first-timers who want beach and river in one weekend.

The route: overnight train to Hubballi, Vibhuthi falls and Murudeshwar on day 1 with a night at Nirvana Beach camp, Gokarna's temple, Mirjan Fort and Om Beach on day 2, then a riverside camp in Dandeli, and Kali river activities on day 3 before the 9:05 pm train back. You're home around 10 am on day 4.

It's also available from [Bangalore](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-bangalore/) and [Vijayawada](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-vijayawada/).

## Gokarna & Dandeli for Couples

[View the trip](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-couple-from-hyderabad/)

**Best for:** couples who want the beach-and-river route on a trip designed for couples.

It follows the group trip's route, leaving on Friday afternoon. The price is shown per person. Read [couple and family weekend getaways](/travel-guides/couple-and-family-weekend-getaways-from-hyderabad/) for what to check before booking.

## Hampi, Gokarna & Murudeshwar

[View the trip](/trips/hampi-gokarna-murudeshwar-from-hyderabad/)

**Best for:** travellers with an extra day who want history as well as beaches.

Three nights by sleeper bus: the ruins of Hampi with a guide and a coracle ride, the Vitthala temple, then the coast at Murudeshwar and Gokarna. Food isn't included, and 5% GST and payment gateway charges are added. Dates are on request.

## Dudhsagar & Dandeli

[View the trip](/trips/dudhsagar-dandeli-from-hyderabad/)

**Best for:** a monsoon trip built around waterfalls rather than beaches.

One night in tents at a Dandeli river camp, then the Dudhsagar falls. It runs in the monsoon; jeep ride charges, rafting and the zipline are extra. See [the best time to visit Gokarna and Dandeli](/travel-guides/best-time-to-visit-gokarna-and-dandeli/) for why the season matters.

## How to choose

- **Most frequent departures:** Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar (Thursday and Friday).
- **Travelling as a couple:** the couple trip (ask on WhatsApp how tents are allotted).
- **More days, more history:** Hampi, Gokarna & Murudeshwar.
- **Monsoon waterfalls:** Dudhsagar & Dandeli.
- **Tightest budget:** compare the live prices in the table above.
