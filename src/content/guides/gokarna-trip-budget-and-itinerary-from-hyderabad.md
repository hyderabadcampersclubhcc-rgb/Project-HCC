Gokarna is a temple town and beach destination on the Karnataka coast. From Hyderabad it's an overnight journey each way, so most people pair it with nearby places to make the trip worth it: Murudeshwar's seaside temple and the river camps of Dandeli. This guide sets out a realistic route, a day-by-day plan and where the money goes. For the season to go, see [the best time to visit Gokarna and Dandeli](/travel-guides/best-time-to-visit-gokarna-and-dandeli/).

## Getting there from Hyderabad

HCC's trips don't go straight to Gokarna; the group travels via **Hubballi**:

1. **Overnight train** from Hyderabad (Nampally) to Hubballi. HCC's trip leaves at 3 pm on Thursday or Friday and reaches Hubballi by about 6:30 am.
2. **Private vehicle** from Hubballi to the coast, then on to Dandeli and back to Hubballi for the return train.

If you're planning independently, compare current train, bus and flight options to Hubballi, Karwar or Goa, and plan your road transfers from there. Schedules change, so check them before you book.

## A 2-night itinerary (the HCC route)

### Day 0: Leave Hyderabad

Board the overnight train at Nampally in the afternoon. Eat before you board or carry dinner.

### Day 1: Vibhuthi falls and Murudeshwar

- Reach Hubballi early and drive towards the coast.
- **Vibhuthi falls:** a short trek and a swim.
- **Honnavar boardwalk**, if time permits.
- **Murudeshwar:** the temple and beach by the evening.
- **Night:** beach camp at Nirvana Beach, Gokarna.

### Day 2: Gokarna and on to Dandeli

- **Mahabaleshwar Temple** at 6 am, before the crowds.
- **Mirjan Fort** on the way.
- **Om Beach**, if time permits.
- **Afternoon:** drive to Dandeli for a rain dance, dinner and a bonfire at the riverside camp.

### Day 3: The Kali river

- **Kayaking, zorbing and river boating**, all included on HCC's trip.
- **Rafting and ziplining** are optional extras, subject to the river on the day.
- **Evening:** back to Hubballi for the 9:05 pm train.

### Day 4: Home

Arrive in Hyderabad around 10 am.

The order of places can change with weather, traffic or local restrictions; on HCC's trips the trip captain decides on the day.

### Planning it yourself

Doing Gokarna independently can work well for a longer, slower trip. The costs to research and add up are:

- **Long-distance travel** to Hubballi, Karwar or Goa and back
- **Road transfers** between the station, Murudeshwar, Gokarna and Dandeli (this is often the expensive and time-consuming part)
- **Stays** in each place
- **River activities** at Dandeli, booked through a camp or licensed operator
- **Food and entry tickets**

A group trip mainly saves the road-transfer planning and the separate bookings. Going independently gives you more time in each place. Choose based on how much time you have.

## Where to stay

On HCC's trip you spend one night at a beach camp at Nirvana Beach in Gokarna and one at a riverside camp in Dandeli, in tents on a sharing basis. If you plan independently, Gokarna has stays in town and near the beaches; book ahead for weekends and late December.

## What to pack

- Government photo ID (needed on trains)
- Comfortable walking shoes and slippers
- Swimwear and a quick-dry towel
- Sunscreen and a cap
- Power bank and personal medicines
- A reusable water bottle

See the full [camping packing list](/travel-guides/what-to-pack-for-a-camping-trip/).

## Good to know

- **Food:** coastal Karnataka food may differ from what you're used to in Hyderabad.
- **Sea safety:** swim only where it's allowed and when conditions are calm.
- **Temple visits:** dress modestly for the Mahabaleshwar and Murudeshwar temples.
- **Children:** on HCC's Gokarna trip, children under 4 travel free and those 5 and above pay the adult price. Ask on WhatsApp about anything in between.
