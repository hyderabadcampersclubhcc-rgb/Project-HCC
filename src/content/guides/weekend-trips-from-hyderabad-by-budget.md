The right weekend trip from Hyderabad usually comes down to two numbers: how many days you can take off, and how much you want to spend. This guide sorts HCC's trips into budget bands so you can see what each level of spend actually buys. Every price below is read live from the trip pages, so it always matches what you would be quoted.

**How to read the prices:** they are per person, on a sharing basis, and cover what the trip page lists as included. Food, entry tickets and optional activities are usually extra.

## Under ₹3,000: one night, close to the city

If you want a night out of the city without taking leave, a one-night camp is the cheapest option.

{{trip-table: ananthagiri-camping, ananthagiri-harivillu-camping, ananthagiri-sarpanpally-lake-camping, ananthagiri-trance-veechika-camping, gandikota-camping | One-night camps (live prices)}}

What separates these camps is not just price:

- **Ananthagiri Hills camps** (near Vikarabad, about 80 km away) are reach-it-yourself. The price covers the tent, meals and activities at the campsite, but not your travel. The budget camp has a pool and a Saturday music night; the higher-priced camps add things like a rope course, a lakeside setting or a guided sunrise trek.
- **Gandikota** includes a vehicle from Hyderabad and back, a tent with sleeping bags and meals, so the price covers almost everything except snacks and personal spending.

For a full comparison of the camps, see [camping near Hyderabad](/camping-near-hyderabad/) and the [Ananthagiri Hills camping guide](/travel-guides/ananthagiri-hills-camping-guide/).

## ₹6,000 to ₹8,000: two nights away with travel included

This is where most weekend group trips from Hyderabad sit. You leave on a Thursday or Friday evening by overnight train, spend two nights away and come back early in the week.

{{trip-table: coorg-chikmagalur, araku-vanajangi-vizag, gokarna-dandeli, ooty-coonoor, isha-ooty-coonoor, wayanad, lonavala-matheran, pondicherry, goa | 2-night trips from Hyderabad (live prices)}}

What this price usually includes:

- Non-AC sleeper train tickets, onward and return (a few trips use a bus; the trip page says which)
- Two nights' stay on a sharing basis (rooms, homestays or camps, depending on the trip)
- A vehicle for local sightseeing, with tolls, parking and driver charges
- A trip captain or guide

What it usually doesn't include: most meals, entry tickets, jeep rides and water sports. On some trips a few meals are included, for example the camp meals on [Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-hyderabad/). The [weekend trips under ₹8,000](/travel-guides/weekend-trips-from-hyderabad-under-8000/) guide has a trip-by-trip table of inclusions.

### Which 2-night trip suits which budget-minded traveller?

- **Hills and coffee country:** [Coorg & Chikmagalur](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-hyderabad/) includes an AC vehicle and a night at a coffee plantation.
- **Closest by train:** [Araku Valley, Vanajangi & Vizag](/trips/araku-vanajangi-vizag-from-hyderabad/) leaves on Thursday evening and combines hills with the Vizag coast.
- **Beach and river camps:** [Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-hyderabad/) puts you in a beach camp and a riverside camp, with kayaking and zorbing included.
- **Classic hill station:** [Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/) or [Wayanad](/trips/wayanad-from-hyderabad/).

## ₹8,000 to ₹15,000: longer trips and special formats

Above ₹8,000 you are usually paying for more days, a houseboat night, or flights or long train journeys to reach the starting city.

{{trip-table: munnar, hampi-gokarna-murudeshwar, golden-triangle, kerala-munnar-alleppey | Longer trips from Hyderabad (live prices)}}

- **[Kerala: Munnar & Alleppey](/trips/kerala-munnar-alleppey-from-hyderabad/)** includes a night on a houseboat, with lunch and dinner on board.
- **[Hampi, Gokarna & Murudeshwar](/trips/hampi-gokarna-murudeshwar-from-hyderabad/)** is a 3-night trip by sleeper bus, with a guided Hampi tour.
- **[Golden Triangle](/trips/golden-triangle-from-hyderabad/)** covers Agra, Jaipur and Delhi over 3 nights. Note that 5% GST applies on this trip.

## Couple trips

Couple trips are designed for two and are priced per person. See [Gokarna & Dandeli for Couples](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-couple-from-hyderabad/) (from {{price: gokarna-dandeli-couple}} per person) and the [couple and family weekend getaways](/travel-guides/couple-and-family-weekend-getaways-from-hyderabad/) guide.

## Costs to plan for beyond the trip price

Whatever band you choose, set aside money for:

1. **Food.** Most trips include only some meals, or none. Check each trip page.
2. **Entry tickets and activities.** Waterfalls, parks, boating, jeep rides and water sports are paid at the spot.
3. **Getting to the start.** For the Ananthagiri camps, that's your fuel or a train to Vikarabad plus an auto. For other trips, the trip starts at the station named on the trip page.
4. **Personal spending.** Snacks, water, shopping and tips.

If you'd like a rough total for a particular trip, message HCC on WhatsApp before you book.
