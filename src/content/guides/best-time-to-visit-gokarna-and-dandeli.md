Gokarna and Dandeli are both in Uttara Kannada, and HCC's trips from Hyderabad combine them in one weekend: beaches and the temple town on one side, forest and the Kali river on the other. Their best seasons overlap, but not completely. This guide explains when each is at its best and how the seasons affect a combined trip.

## The short answer

| Months | Gokarna (beaches) | Dandeli (river and forest) | Combined trip |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| October to February | Best: calm sea, pleasant days | Good: river camps and water activities running | **Best** |
| March | Good, getting warmer | Good | Good |
| April and May | Hot and humid | Good, warmer | Possible, plan for heat |
| June to September | Monsoon: rough sea, unsafe to swim | Lush, but activities depend on water levels | Not ideal for beaches |

These are general patterns, not guarantees. Weather varies from year to year, so check the forecast close to your date.

## Gokarna by season

### October to March: beach season

After the monsoon, the sea calms down and the beaches become what most people come for. Om Beach, the best-known of them, is on HCC's Gokarna itinerary when time permits. Mornings are good for the Mahabaleshwar Temple in town, which HCC groups visit early, at 6 am, before the crowds.

Late December, around Christmas and New Year, is usually the busiest stretch. If you want quieter beaches, November, January and February are calmer.

### April and May: hot and humid

The beaches are still open, but the midday heat is strong on the coast. Plan beach time for early morning and late afternoon, carry water, and use sunscreen.

### June to September: monsoon

The Karnataka coast gets heavy rain in the monsoon. The sea is rough and swimming is unsafe. Paths between beaches can be slippery. The town and temple remain open, and the landscape turns green, but it's not a beach holiday.

## Dandeli by season

### October to February: the classic river season

The forest is still green after the rains, nights are cooler, and river camps run their activities. On HCC's [Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-hyderabad/) trip, kayaking, zorbing and river boating are included at the camp. Rafting and ziplining are optional extras.

### March to May: warmer, still good

Dandeli works into the summer. It's warmer, but the river is the main attraction and the camps keep running.

### June to September: monsoon

The forest is at its greenest. River activities, especially rafting, depend on water levels in the Kali, which are controlled by dam releases, and they usually pause during the peak monsoon for safety. If you want monsoon waterfalls, HCC's [Dudhsagar & Dandeli](/trips/dudhsagar-dandeli-from-hyderabad/) trip is built around the Dudhsagar falls; its dates come out when the rains start.

## Season tips

- **Pack for the season.** October to February: light clothes, a light layer for the river camp at night and swimwear. April and May: sun protection and a cap. Monsoon: a rain jacket and quick-dry clothes.
- **Respect sea warnings.** Even in season, swim only where it's allowed and when lifeguards or local guidance say it's safe.
- **Ask about rafting before you go.** It's optional on HCC's trips and depends on the river on the day.
- **Book late December early.** It's the busiest period on the coast, so seats fill sooner.
