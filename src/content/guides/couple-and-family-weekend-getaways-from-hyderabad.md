Couples and families want different things from a weekend away. Couples usually want privacy, a room of their own and a slower pace. Families need easy logistics, sensible travel times and clear answers about children. This guide covers HCC's options for both, and the questions worth asking before you pay.

## Couple trips

HCC runs couple versions of popular trips. They follow a similar route to the group version but are designed for couples; check each trip page for the accommodation.

{{trip-table: gokarna-dandeli-couple, ooty-coonoor-couple | Couple trips from Hyderabad (live prices, per person)}}

### Gokarna & Dandeli for Couples

[Gokarna & Dandeli for Couples](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-couple-from-hyderabad/) follows the beach-and-river route: an overnight train to Hubballi, waterfalls and Murudeshwar, a night by the beach in Gokarna and a night at a riverside camp in Dandeli. Water activities, a bonfire and entry tickets are included, along with one breakfast, one lunch and one dinner. Rafting and ziplining are optional extras. It leaves Nampally on Friday afternoon. Ask on WhatsApp how tents are allotted for couples.

### Ooty & Coonoor for Couples

[Ooty & Coonoor for Couples](/trips/ooty-coonoor-couple-from-hyderabad/) is the quieter choice: tea gardens, Coonoor's viewpoints and the pine forest, with a couple room and local transport. Food, toy train tickets and entry tickets are paid separately. Dates are shared on request, so ask on WhatsApp.

### Kerala Honeymoon (from Kochi)

[Kerala Honeymoon](/trips/kerala-honeymoon-from-kochi/) is a private trip that starts and ends in Kochi: Athirappilly falls, a hotel in Munnar and a private houseboat in Alleppey, with a private cab throughout. Flights or trains to Kochi are not included, so add those to your budget. See the [Kerala trip planning guide](/travel-guides/kerala-trip-planning-guide-from-hyderabad/) for timing.

### What couples should check before booking

- **Room or tent arrangement.** Confirm it on WhatsApp for your departure.
- **Train berths.** On train trips you travel in sleeper class with the group; berths are allotted with the tickets.
- **Meals.** Most trips include few or no meals. Plan for dinners out.
- **ID.** Some stays check ID for every guest. One Ananthagiri campsite (Harivillu Resort) does not admit unmarried couples under the resort's own rules, so check camp rules before you choose a camp.

## Family weekend getaways

There's no dedicated family package from Hyderabad at the moment, but several trips suit families well. What matters most is pace and travel time.

### One night, close to home

The [Ananthagiri Hills camps](/camping-near-hyderabad/) are about 80 km from Hyderabad, so you can drive there in the afternoon and be home by lunchtime the next day. The budget camp has a pool, games and a buffet, which keeps children busy. You drive yourselves, which also means you can leave whenever you need to.

### Easy-paced 2-night trips

If you're comfortable with an overnight train, these have fewer long transfers:

- **[Wayanad](/trips/wayanad-from-hyderabad/):** two nights at one homestay, so no daily packing.
- **[Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/):** two nights in Ooty, with local sightseeing each day.

### A longer family holiday

The [Golden Triangle](/trips/golden-triangle-from-hyderabad/) covers Agra, Jaipur and Delhi over 3 nights in standard hotels, with dinners included. Dates are on request, and 5% GST applies.

### Questions to ask before booking with children

1. **Can children join this trip, and at what price?** This varies, so HCC confirms it on WhatsApp.
2. **How many hours are in the vehicle each day?** Look at the itinerary on the trip page.
3. **Are there long walks or climbs?** Viewpoints like Mullayanagiri involve steps; waterfalls may involve slippery paths.
4. **What are the sleeping arrangements?** Shared rooms on group trips are set up for adults travelling together; ask what works for a family.
5. **What food is available?** If your child has dietary needs, ask in advance.
