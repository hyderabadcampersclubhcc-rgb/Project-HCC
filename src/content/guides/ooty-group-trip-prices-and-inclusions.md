## Ooty & Coonoor

[View the trip](/trips/ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/)

The standard group trip, running on Thursdays and Fridays. It's an experience-focused budget trip covering many places in a short time: Bandipur, Pykara falls and Shooting Point on day 1, Coonoor's tea country and the toy train (if available) on day 2, and the pine forest on day 3. Male and female accommodation arrangements are made in the shared rooms.

**Good to know:** the boarding point, departure time and return travel details are confirmed on WhatsApp for your date.

## Isha, Ooty & Coonoor

[View the trip](/trips/isha-ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/)

Adds the Isha Yoga Centre to the Nilgiris. It leaves Hyderabad at 7 pm on Thursdays and Fridays by sleeper train to Mysore, and you're back in Hyderabad around 6 am on Monday or Tuesday.

## Ooty & Coonoor for Couples

[View the trip](/trips/ooty-coonoor-couple-from-hyderabad/)

The quieter version for two, with a couple room. Dates are on request; ask on WhatsApp.

## Wayanad, Ooty & Mysore

[View the trip](/trips/wayanad-ooty-mysore-from-hyderabad/)

The longest option, with 4 nights: Wayanad's dam, falls and glass bridge, the drive through Mudumalai to Ooty, Coonoor by toy train (ticket included) and Mysore on the way home. Dates are on request.

## Extra costs to budget for

Whichever trip you choose, plan for:

1. **Food,** three meals a day
2. **Entry tickets** at falls, gardens, viewpoints and the lake
3. **The toy train ticket** (except on Wayanad, Ooty & Mysore), if seats are available
4. **Activities** such as boating at Ooty Lake
5. **Personal spending** on tea, chocolate and shopping

Ask HCC on WhatsApp if you'd like help estimating these for your trip.

## Which one should you book?

- **Most departures:** Ooty & Coonoor.
- **Want to visit Isha:** Isha, Ooty & Coonoor.
- **Travelling as a couple:** Ooty & Coonoor for Couples.
- **Have 5 days and want the toy train included:** Wayanad, Ooty & Mysore.
