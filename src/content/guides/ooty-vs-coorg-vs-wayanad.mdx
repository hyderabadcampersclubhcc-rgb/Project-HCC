Ooty, Coorg and Wayanad are close neighbours on the map. HCC's trips to all three leave Hyderabad by overnight train to Mysore and spend two nights in the hills. The difference is what you do once you're there.

## The short answer

- **Choose Ooty & Coonoor** for the classic hill station: Mysore Palace, the drive through Bandipur forest, Pykara falls and the Nilgiri toy train to Coonoor.
- **Choose Coorg & Chikmagalur** for coffee country: Abbey Falls, Mandalpatti, a night at a coffee plantation and a 5 am sunrise climb at Mullayanagiri, Karnataka's highest peak.
- **Choose Wayanad** for treks and activities: the Chembra Peak trek, a zipline, the 900 Kandi glass bridge and the Kuruva Dweep river islands.

## Ooty & Coonoor: the classic hill station

[Ooty](/destinations/ooty/) and Coorg & Chikmagalur are rated easy; Wayanad is rated moderate. Day one combines Mysore Palace and Chamundi Hill with the drive up through Bandipur forest to Needle Rock, Pykara falls and Shooting Point. Day two is the Nilgiri Mountain Railway toy train from Ooty to Coonoor, with Dolphin's Nose and a tea-factory visit. The last morning is in the Pine Forest. There is also a version with the Adiyogi light show at the Isha Yoga Center: [Isha, Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/isha-ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/).

## Coorg & Chikmagalur: coffee country and a sunrise peak

[Coorg & Chikmagalur](/destinations/coorg/) packs two coffee districts into one trip. You see Abbey Falls, Mandalpatti and Raja's Seat, spend a night on a coffee plantation, then cross to [Chikmagalur](/destinations/chikmagalur/) via the Dubare elephant camp, the Belur temple and water sports at Yagachi dam. The early start for the Mullayanagiri sunrise is the highlight. It also runs from Bangalore by Tempo Traveller on Fridays and Saturdays.

<TripCallout trip="coorg-chikmagalur" />

## Wayanad: treks and adventure

[Wayanad](/destinations/wayanad/) is built around activities: the Chembra Peak trek, a zipline, the glass bridge, Banasura Sagar dam and Soochipara falls, with nights in a homestay. It leaves only on Thursdays, so you need Friday off.
