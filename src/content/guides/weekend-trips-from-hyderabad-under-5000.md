A ₹5,000 budget won't cover two nights away with train tickets on HCC's trips, but it comfortably covers a night under canvas, often with money left over. This guide covers what you can do from Hyderabad for under ₹5,000 per person, what each option actually includes, and the costs that sit outside the price.

## The options under ₹5,000

{{trip-table: ananthagiri-camping, ananthagiri-harivillu-camping, ananthagiri-sarpanpally-lake-camping, ananthagiri-trance-veechika-camping, gandikota-camping | Camps under ₹5,000 (live prices)}}

All five are one-night trips. They fall into two types.

### Reach-it-yourself camps at Ananthagiri Hills

The four Ananthagiri campsites are near Vikarabad, about 80 km from Hyderabad. You get yourself there, so the price covers only what happens at the camp:

- **[Budget Camping with Pool & Music](/trips/ananthagiri-camping-from-hyderabad/)**: a tent for 2 or 3 with bedding, evening tea and snacks, a dinner buffet and breakfast, the swimming pool (5 to 10 pm), games, and a DJ session on Saturdays.
- **[Premium Adventure Camping (Harivillu Resort)](/trips/ananthagiri-harivillu-camping-from-hyderabad/)**: 2-sharing tents, a rope course of about 10 activities, cycling, a short trek, sports, a bonfire and an open-air movie if the weather allows.
- **[Lakeside Camping (Sarpanpally Lake)](/trips/ananthagiri-sarpanpally-lake-camping-from-hyderabad/)**: 2-sharing tents on the lake bank, a bonfire hour, games and a walk to an organic farm and petting zoo.
- **[Premium Camping (Trance Veechika Resort)](/trips/ananthagiri-trance-veechika-camping-from-hyderabad/)**: tents for 2 or 3, a pool session, sand volleyball, a bonfire and a guided sunrise trek.

### Gandikota: transport included

[Gandikota Canyon Camping](/trips/gandikota-camping-from-hyderabad/) works differently. A non-AC vehicle leaves Hyderabad at 6 am, and the price includes the return drive, a tent with sleeping bags, four meals and a trek leader. It's a long drive for one night, but you don't need your own transport. Read the [Gandikota camping guide](/travel-guides/gandikota-camping-guide/) before you choose it.

## Working out your real total

The camp price is the biggest cost, but not the only one. Count these before you decide:

| Cost | Ananthagiri camps | Gandikota |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Getting there | Your fuel or a train to Vikarabad plus an auto or cab | Included |
| Meals | Included as listed on each camp page | 4 meals included |
| Extra activities | Some camps have paid extras; ask on WhatsApp | Not applicable |
| Snacks and drinks | Your own | Your own |

**Getting to Ananthagiri without a car:** train 12702 leaves Nampally at 2:55 pm (via Begumpet and Lingampalli) and reaches Vikarabad at 4:02 pm, and train 12732 leaves Secunderabad at 4:00 pm and reaches at 5:15 pm. From Vikarabad station, an auto or cab covers the last stretch. Check current train times with Indian Railways before you travel.

## How to keep the total low

1. **Pick the camp, not the extras.** The budget Ananthagiri camp already includes the pool, games and three meals.
2. **Share the drive.** If a few of you are going, one car split four ways is often cheaper than individual train and auto fares.
3. **Carry your own snacks and water** (where camp rules allow outside food; some campsites don't, so check the camp page).
4. **Go on a weekday-friendly plan.** Camps run on set days, so ask on WhatsApp which dates are open before you plan around them.

## What you won't get for under ₹5,000

To set expectations: these are organised campsites with shared washrooms, not resorts. You won't get private rooms, and on the Ananthagiri camps you won't get transport. If you want two nights away with train travel, budget about ₹6,000 to ₹8,000; see [weekend trips by budget](/travel-guides/weekend-trips-from-hyderabad-by-budget/).

## Booking a camp

Camps have limited tents, so check availability on WhatsApp first. A ₹500 non-refundable advance per person holds your place. For the budget Ananthagiri camp, the remaining amount is paid after you reach the campsite. For the other camps, the balance deadline is confirmed on WhatsApp. The full terms are in the [cancellation policy](/cancellation-policy/).

## Which one should you choose?

- **Lowest total cost:** the budget Ananthagiri camp, especially if you can share a car.
- **Most activities:** Harivillu, for the rope course.
- **Quietest setting:** Sarpanpally, by the lake.
- **No car and want everything arranged:** Gandikota.
