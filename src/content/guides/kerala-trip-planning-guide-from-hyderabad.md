Kerala rewards a little planning. The places most first-timers want to see (the tea hills of Munnar, the backwaters around Alleppey, the cliffs at Varkala) are spread out, and road travel between them takes time. This guide helps you decide how many days you need, which places to include, when to go and how to get there from Hyderabad.

## Step 2: Choose your places

### Munnar: tea hills

Munnar is the hill station: tea estates, a tea factory, Top Station and Echo Point, Mattupetty Dam and Eravikulam National Park, home of the Nilgiri tahr. It's cool, green and good for a slower pace. More on the [Munnar destination page](/destinations/munnar/).

### Alleppey: backwaters

Alleppey (Alappuzha) is the backwater base. The classic experience is a night on a houseboat: check in around lunchtime, cruise the lake, dinner on board, and a morning cruise through the canals before check-out. Marari Beach is nearby. More on the [Alleppey destination page](/destinations/alleppey/).

### Varkala and Trivandrum: the coast

Varkala is known for its cliff above the beach. On HCC's 6-day trip, the group stops at the Jatayu Earth's Center on the way, then spends time at Varkala and Trivandrum before the train home.

### Athirappilly: waterfalls

Athirappilly falls is on the way from Kochi towards Munnar, and features on the Kerala Honeymoon and the Kerala trips that start in Kochi.

## Step 3: Pick your season

- **September to March:** the main season. Post-monsoon green in the hills, and the backwaters are pleasant.
- **April and May:** hotter and more humid on the coast and backwaters; Munnar stays cooler.
- **June to August:** the southwest monsoon usually reaches Kerala in early June. Very green, but heavy rain, slippery paths and occasional disruptions.

## Step 4: Plan the route from Hyderabad

- **To Munnar:** HCC's Munnar trip goes by train to Coimbatore (arriving about 8 am), then by road through the Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary.
- **To Munnar and Alleppey:** HCC's Kerala trips take a non-AC sleeper train to Kochi, drive up to Munnar first, then down to Alleppey for the houseboat night.
- **Kerala Honeymoon:** starts with a pickup in Kochi and ends with a drop at Kochi airport. Flights or trains to Kochi are your own.

Road journeys in Kerala's hills are slow. Don't plan Munnar and Alleppey on the same day if you're travelling independently.

## Step 5: Budget

The trip price covers different things on each Kerala trip, so compare them on the trip pages. As an example, **Kerala: Munnar & Alleppey** includes:

- Non-AC sleeper train tickets, Hyderabad–Kochi and back
- Hotels on triple or quad sharing, plus one houseboat night
- Daily breakfast, and lunch and dinner on the houseboat
- Local transport for four days, with tolls, parking and permits

Plan separately for other meals, entry charges, boating, jeep safaris and personal expenses. The 6-day trip includes dinner on the houseboat but not other meals.

## What to pack

- Light layers plus a warmer layer for Munnar mornings
- Comfortable shoes and slippers
- Rain jacket or umbrella (useful in any season in Kerala)
- Sunscreen and a cap for the coast
- Government photo ID
- Personal medicines and insect repellent

## Planning tips

- **Book the houseboat night early** if you're going independently in peak season.
- **Keep one buffer.** Hill roads and weather can slow you down.
- **Respect wildlife rules** at Eravikulam and Chinnar; follow the park's instructions.
- **Group trips run on set dates.** HCC's longer Kerala trips run on dates given on request, so message on WhatsApp early.
