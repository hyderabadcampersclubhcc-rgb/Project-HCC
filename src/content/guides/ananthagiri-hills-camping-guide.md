Ananthagiri Hills, near Vikarabad, is a forest getaway about 80 km from Hyderabad: close enough to leave after lunch and be back before Sunday lunch. HCC works with four campsites here. This guide covers the practical side of a visit: getting there, the season, what a camp night looks like, what's nearby and the rules to know. To compare the four campsites side by side, see the [Ananthagiri Hills camp comparison](/camping-near-hyderabad/ananthagiri-hills/), and for prices and facilities, [camping near Hyderabad: costs and facilities](/travel-guides/camping-near-hyderabad-costs-and-facilities/).

## Getting there from Hyderabad

### By car or bike

About 80 km, roughly 2 to 2.5 hours. Leave early enough to arrive before check-in; late arrivals can miss meals and activities. At the budget camp, parking is free, away from the tents, at the owner's risk.

### By train and auto

- **Train 12702** from Nampally at 2:55 pm (also stopping at Begumpet at 3:05 pm and Lingampalli at 3:30 pm) reaches Vikarabad at 4:02 pm.
- **Train 12732** from Secunderabad at 4:00 pm reaches Vikarabad at 5:15 pm.
- From Vikarabad station, take an **auto or cab** to the campsite. The budget camp is about 9 km from the station.

Train timings can change, so check them with Indian Railways before you travel.

### By bus

TSRTC buses run to Vikarabad. From the bus stand, take an auto or cab to the campsite.

## Best time to go

**July to February.** The hills turn green after the monsoon arrives, and nights are cooler from October. The monsoon months are the greenest but can be wet; carry a raincoat or poncho. March to June is hotter, and the pool camps are the most comfortable choice then.

## How a camp night works

Timings vary by camp; this is the budget camp's schedule as an example.

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 4:00 pm | Check in, tent allotment |
| 5 to 6 pm | Tea and hot snacks |
| 5 to 10 pm | Pool open |
| From 8 pm (Saturdays) | DJ music session |
| 8:30 to 10 pm | Dinner buffet |
| 10 pm | Campsite gate closes |
| Next day, 8:30 am | Breakfast |
| By 11 am | Check out |

At the Trance Veechika camp, the guided sunrise trek starts at 6 am. Check-in times for Harivillu, Sarpanpally and Trance Veechika: Contact us on WhatsApp to confirm.

## Nearby places (on your own)

If you have your own transport, these are worth a stop before or after the camp:

- **Kotpalli Lake:** boating and kayaking.
- **Anantha Padmanabha Swamy Temple.**
- **Ananthagiri viewpoint:** forest views.
- **Damagundam Shiva Temple:** a temple in the forest.

Entry, boating and parking at these places are paid on the spot and aren't part of the camp price.

## What to carry

- Government photo ID for every guest
- Comfortable shoes (trekking or sports shoes if you'll walk)
- Extra clothes for the pool or rain dance, and slippers
- Toothbrush, paste, soap and towel
- Torch and power bank
- Light jacket or hoodie for cooler nights
- Raincoat or poncho in the monsoon

Full list: [what to pack for a camping trip](/travel-guides/what-to-pack-for-a-camping-trip/).

## Camp rules to know

- Check availability before you go; there are no walk-ins.
- Arrive on time. Late arrivals may miss meals or activities.
- Keep your valuables with you.
- No drugs, illegal activity or nuisance; rule-breaking can mean removal without a refund.
- At the budget camp, alcohol from other states and hookah are not allowed, and damage is charged at actuals.
- Harivillu Resort does not permit unmarried couples (a resort rule).

## Booking
