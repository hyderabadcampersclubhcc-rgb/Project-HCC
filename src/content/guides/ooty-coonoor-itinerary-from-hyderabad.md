Ooty and Coonoor are the two best-known towns in the Nilgiris, about an hour apart. Ooty is the bigger hill station, with the lake, pine forest and Pykara falls; Coonoor is smaller and quieter, surrounded by tea estates. Together they make a full, fairly fast-paced 3-day trip. This guide sets out a realistic day-by-day plan based on HCC's group itinerary, with timing tips. For prices and what each Ooty package includes, see [Ooty group trip prices and inclusions](/travel-guides/ooty-group-trip-prices-and-inclusions/).

## How the trip starts from Hyderabad

There's no direct rail link from Hyderabad to Ooty, so the trip begins with an overnight journey to a city at the foot of the hills, then road:

- **[Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/):** non-AC sleeper train from Hyderabad to Bangalore on Thursday or Friday, then road to Ooty. The boarding point, departure time and return travel details are confirmed on WhatsApp for your date.
- **[Isha, Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/isha-ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/):** overnight sleeper train to Mysore at 7 pm on Thursday or Friday, then road; this version adds the Isha Yoga Centre near Coimbatore.

## Day 1: Through Bandipur to Ooty

- **Early start** (6 am on HCC's trip). Freshen up on the way; there's no shower stop.
- **Bandipur forest drive:** the road to the Nilgiris runs through the forest. Wildlife sightings are possible but not guaranteed.
- **Eucalyptus groves** on the way up.
- **Pykara falls** and **Shooting Point**, the grassy hills used in many film shoots.
- **Evening:** check in at the stay in Ooty. Hill evenings are cool, so keep a layer handy.

**Tip:** the drive is long. Carry water and snacks, and take motion-sickness tablets if you need them on hill roads.

## Day 2: Coonoor and tea country

- **6 am start.**
- **Dolphin Nose Point or Doddabetta:** one of the two viewpoints, depending on the day.
- **Tea gardens:** a walk through the estates.
- **Tea factory and chocolate factory.**
- **Nilgiri toy train from Ooty to Coonoor,** subject to availability. The Nilgiri Mountain Railway is a UNESCO World Heritage site, and seats are limited, so it can't be promised. The ticket is paid separately.
- **Ooty Lake boathouse** and **shopping** in Ooty in the evening.

**Tip:** if the toy train isn't available, the drive between Ooty and Coonoor is scenic in its own right.

## Day 3: Pine forest and down to Mysore

- **6 am start.**
- **Pine Forest** and **Valley View Point.**
- **Drive back to Mysore,** with Mysore Palace only if time permits.
- **Journey home.**

The itinerary can change with weather, road conditions or availability; on HCC's trips the organiser decides when changes are needed for safety or operational reasons.

## What a 3-day plan leaves out

Ooty has more than three days of sights. If you have extra time, consider a slower day in Coonoor, the botanical gardens in Ooty, or adding Wayanad. HCC's [Wayanad, Ooty & Mysore](/trips/wayanad-ooty-mysore-from-hyderabad/) is a 4-night version that includes toy train tickets.

## What to pack

- Light jacket or hoodie (more in winter)
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Government photo ID
- Power bank, water bottle, personal medicines

## Is this itinerary right for you?

It's an experience-focused budget trip: many places in a short time, with long travel hours. If you want a relaxed hill-station break, choose a trip with fewer stops, such as [Wayanad](/trips/wayanad-from-hyderabad/), or compare the options in [Ooty vs Coorg vs Wayanad](/travel-guides/ooty-vs-coorg-vs-wayanad/).
