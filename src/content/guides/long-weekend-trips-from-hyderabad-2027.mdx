A long weekend is the easiest way to fit a proper trip into a working year. This guide lists the 2027 holidays that fall next to a weekend, how much leave each one needs, and how HCC's weekly batches from Hyderabad fit around them.

**HCC hasn't published its 2027 departure dates yet.** Everything below is based on the current schedule, so confirm the date on the [upcoming trips page](/upcoming-trips/) before you apply for leave.

## How HCC's Thursday and Friday batches work

Four of HCC's weekend trips from Hyderabad leave twice a week, and the batch you pick decides which extra day you need:

- **Thursday batch:** leaves Thursday evening and is back early on Monday. You need Friday off.
- **Friday batch:** leaves Friday evening (3 pm for Gokarna) and is back early on Tuesday. You need Monday off.

That makes the rule simple: **a Friday holiday suits a Thursday batch, and a Monday holiday suits a Friday batch.**

## 2027 holidays that make a long weekend

| Holiday | Date in 2027 | Day | Long weekend? |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Year's Day | 1 January | Friday | Yes: Friday to Sunday |
| Republic Day | 26 January | Tuesday | Take Monday off for four days |
| Good Friday | 26 March | Friday | Yes: Friday to Sunday |
| Telangana Formation Day | 2 June | Wednesday | No |
| Independence Day | 15 August | Sunday | No |
| Gandhi Jayanti | 2 October | Saturday | No |
| Christmas | 25 December | Saturday | No |

Festival holidays (Sankranti, Ugadi, Ramzan, Bakrid, Vinayaka Chavithi, Dussehra, Diwali and others) follow the lunar calendar. Their 2027 dates aren't in this table: check the Telangana government's official holiday list or your company's list, then use the Thursday/Friday rule above.

### New Year 2027

New Year's Day 2027 is a Friday, so a **Thursday batch on 31 December 2026** covers it, with no leave needed beyond Thursday evening. These Thursday departures are already on sale: see [Coorg & Chikmagalur](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-hyderabad/?d=2026-12-31), [Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/?d=2026-12-31) and [Gokarna & Dandeli](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-hyderabad/?d=2026-12-31). HCC's Thursday-only trips to [Wayanad](/trips/wayanad-from-hyderabad/) and [Araku](/trips/araku-vanajangi-vizag-from-hyderabad/) also have a 31 December departure.

### Republic Day (Tuesday 26 January)

Take Monday 25 January off for a four-day break. On the current pattern, a **Friday batch**, if HCC runs one that week, would leave on Friday 22 January and be back early on the holiday itself.

### Good Friday (Friday 26 March)

Good Friday gives a three-day weekend with no leave. A **Thursday batch** leaving on 25 March would fit it, if HCC runs its usual schedule that week.

## Which trip for which long weekend?

- **Hills and cool weather:** [Ooty & Coonoor](/destinations/ooty/) or [Coorg & Chikmagalur](/destinations/coorg/), both by overnight train via Mysore.
- **Beaches and river camping:** [Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar](/destinations/gokarna/), with a beach camp and a riverside camp.
- **One night, close to the city:** [camping near Hyderabad](/camping-near-hyderabad/) at Narsapur or Ananthagiri Hills, which needs no leave at all.
