From October to February, most places within an overnight journey of Hyderabad are at their best: the monsoon has passed, the hills are green and cool, and the coast is dry enough for beach nights. These are HCC's weekend trips that suit the season, with live dates and prices.

## Hills and coffee country

Cool evenings and clear views make winter the easiest time for the hills. All four go by overnight sleeper train, so you wake up at the start of the trip.

<TripTable trips={['ooty-coonoor', 'isha-ooty-coonoor', 'coorg-chikmagalur', 'wayanad']} caption="Hill trips from Hyderabad (live prices)" />

- **[Ooty & Coonoor](/destinations/ooty/):** Mysore Palace, the drive through Bandipur forest, Pykara falls and the Nilgiri toy train to Coonoor. The Ooty guide describes October to June as generally good, with cool evenings.
- **[Coorg & Chikmagalur](/destinations/coorg/):** Abbey Falls, Mandalpatti, a night at a coffee plantation and sunrise at Mullayanagiri, Karnataka's highest peak. October to March is the dry season there.
- **[Wayanad](/destinations/wayanad/):** the Chembra Peak trek, Banasura Sagar dam and the 900 Kandi glass bridge, leaving on Thursdays.

Packing: the trip pages ask for a light jacket or hoodie because hill evenings get cool.

## The Eastern Ghats: Araku and the Vanajangi sunrise

[Araku](/destinations/araku/) is a winter trip in particular. The Vanajangi sunrise above a sea of clouds needs cool, clear mornings, and September to February is the best time for it. HCC's [Araku, Vanajangi & Vizag trip](/trips/araku-vanajangi-vizag-from-hyderabad/) leaves Kacheguda on Thursdays at 5:30 pm.

## Beaches, rivers and ruins

October to March is the best time for Gokarna's beaches, with calmer seas and pleasant weather, so winter is the season for camping by the sea and the river.

- **[Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar](/destinations/gokarna/):** a beach camp at Nirvana Beach, then a riverside camp in Dandeli with kayaking on the Kali. It leaves on Thursdays and Fridays at 3 pm.
- **[Hampi](/destinations/hampi/):** October to February is the best time for walking among the ruins, before March to May gets very hot. HCC's [Hampi, Gokarna & Murudeshwar trip](/trips/hampi-gokarna-murudeshwar-from-hyderabad/) runs by sleeper bus.

## One night close to the city: winter camping

If you can't take leave, a one-night camp near Hyderabad is the shortest winter break. Two options:

- **Ananthagiri Hills:** four campsites near Vikarabad, from a pool and music night to a premium camp with a sunrise trek. See [camping near Hyderabad](/camping-near-hyderabad/).
- **Gandikota:** an overnight camp at the Gandikota canyon, with transport from Hyderabad.

## Planning a winter weekend

- **Leave:** Thursday batches need Friday off, and Friday batches need Monday off. Camping needs none.
- **Dates:** December weekends, Christmas and New Year include Thursday batches on 24 and 31 December. See the [upcoming trips page](/upcoming-trips/) for every date.
- **More options:** [weekend trips from Hyderabad](/weekend-trips-from-hyderabad/) lists every trip, by season and travel style.
