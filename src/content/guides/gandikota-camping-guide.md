Gandikota is one of the most dramatic landscapes within a day's drive of Hyderabad: steep rock walls dropping to the Pennar river, with an old fort on the hill above. The nickname "Grand Canyon of India" is about the look rather than the scale, but standing on the rim at sunrise, it's easy to see why it stuck. This guide covers what Gandikota is, how a camping trip from Hyderabad works, when to go and what to carry.

## What Gandikota is

- **The gorge.** The Pennar (Penna) river has cut a deep, narrow gorge through the Erramala hills here. The name combines the Telugu words for gorge (*gandi*) and fort (*kota*).
- **The fort.** Gandikota Fort stands on the hill above the gorge. Inside its walls are old temples, including the Madhava and Ranganatha temples, and a mosque. The fort's exact founding date is debated; the Kadapa district administration dates it to the 12th century.
- **Where it is.** Gandikota is in Kadapa district, Andhra Pradesh, about 15 km from the town of Jammalamadugu.

### Day 1: Drive to the canyon

- **6 am:** leave Hyderabad in a non-AC vehicle. The drive is about 7 hours (roughly 390 km).
- **Afternoon:** lunch on arrival, then explore the canyon rim.
- **Evening:** bonfire, barbecue and dinner at camp. The night is in tents, with common washrooms.

### Day 2: Sunrise, fort and home

- **Sunrise over the gorge.**
- **Breakfast,** then trekking and rock climbing with the trek leader.
- **Gandikota Fort** and the Pennar river.
- **Lunch,** then the drive back to Hyderabad, arriving at night.

The order can change with weather, traffic or local restrictions; the trip captain decides on the day.

## Best time to go

| Months | Conditions |
| --- | --- |
| October to February | Best: cooler days and nights, comfortable for trekking and sunrise |
| March to June | Very hot in the rocky canyon; midday is hard going |
| July to September | Monsoon: greener, but paths can be slippery and plans weather-dependent |

## What to carry

- Government photo ID
- Trekking shoes with good grip (the rocks can be loose)
- Torch with spare batteries
- A jacket for the night
- Cap, sunscreen and insect repellent
- Full-sleeve clothes for sun protection
- A refillable water bottle and some snacks for the drive

Full list: [what to pack for a camping trip](/travel-guides/what-to-pack-for-a-camping-trip/).

## Safety tips at the canyon

- **Stay back from the edge,** especially for photos and in wind. There are steep drops with no railings in places.
- **Follow the trek leader** on the rocks; don't take shortcuts.
- **Drink water regularly.** The canyon gets hot even in the cooler months.
- **Don't swim in the river** unless your trek leader says a spot is safe.
