Travelling alone doesn't mean planning everything alone. On an HCC group trip from Hyderabad, the travel, stays and day plan are arranged, and you join a group of other travellers at the departure point.

## How joining on your own works

- **You meet the group at the start.** Most weekend trips leave from a railway station in Hyderabad, such as Kacheguda for Ooty & Coonoor, Coorg & Chikmagalur and Araku, or Nampally for Gokarna. The trip page shows the boarding point and time.
- **A trip captain is with the group.** On HCC group trips, an HCC trip captain travels with the group throughout and makes the on-the-day calls, such as the sightseeing order or weather changes.
- **Rooms are shared.** Stays are usually on a sharing basis (3/4 sharing on several trips, tents on camping trips).
- **The price is per person.** Coming alone costs the same as coming with friends.
- **Groups have a set size.** Several trips list their group size on the trip page, for example 10 to 20 people on Coorg & Chikmagalur and Wayanad, or 10 to 30 on Ooty & Coonoor.

## Good first trips for solo travellers

These trips run on fixed weekly days, so you can book a date and turn up. Each one's trip page answers "Can I join if I'm travelling alone?" with a yes.

- **[Ooty & Coonoor](/destinations/ooty/):** an easy first trip: the toy train, viewpoints and hotel or homestay stays on sharing.
- **[Coorg & Chikmagalur](/destinations/coorg/):** coffee estates, a plantation night and an early sunrise climb at Mullayanagiri.
- **[Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar](/destinations/gokarna/):** two camp nights, a bonfire and river activities, with three camp meals included.
- **[Araku, Vanajangi & Vizag](/destinations/araku/):** a bonfire and music night, and the early Vanajangi sunrise walk.

Not sure which hill trip to pick? Compare them in [Ooty vs Coorg vs Wayanad](/travel-guides/ooty-vs-coorg-vs-wayanad/). On a budget? See [weekend trips under ₹8,000](/travel-guides/weekend-trips-from-hyderabad-under-8000/).

## Trips that work differently

- **Couples trips** (Ooty & Coonoor for Couples, Gokarna & Dandeli for Couples) are sold per couple, so they aren't meant for solo travellers.
- **One-night camps in the Ananthagiri Hills:** you can come alone, but you travel to the campsite yourself and meet the group there. See [camping near Hyderabad](/camping-near-hyderabad/).

## Before you book

1. **Ask on WhatsApp** about the group for your date, the room sharing or anything else. The HCC team replies before you pay.
2. **Read the trip page's packing list and "Good to know" notes.** Carry a government photo ID for the train.
3. **Pay the advance** to hold your seat: ₹2,000 per person (₹500 on the camping trips), non-refundable. Read the [cancellation policy](/cancellation-policy/) first.
