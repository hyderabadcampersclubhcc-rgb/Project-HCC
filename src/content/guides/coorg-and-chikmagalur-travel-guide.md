Coorg (Kodagu) and Chikmagalur (Chikkamagaluru) sit next to each other in the Western Ghats of Karnataka, and both grow coffee. They are often visited together, but they feel different: Coorg is about waterfalls, viewpoints and a famous Tibetan monastery, while Chikmagalur is about high grassland ridges, Karnataka's tallest peak and old Hoysala temples. This guide covers what each is known for, when to go and how the route works from Hyderabad. If you want to compare packages, see [Coorg packages from Hyderabad](/travel-guides/coorg-packages-from-hyderabad/).

## Coorg at a glance

- **Abbey Falls:** Coorg's best-known waterfall, near Madikeri.
- **Mandalpatti:** a viewpoint above the clouds on a clear morning, usually reached by jeep.
- **Raja's Seat:** a garden viewpoint in Madikeri, popular at sunset.
- **Namdroling Monastery (the Golden Temple), Bylakuppe:** a large Tibetan Buddhist monastery with a golden prayer hall.
- **Dubare elephant camp:** on the Kaveri, between Coorg and the road to Chikmagalur.
- **Coffee and spices:** local shops sell coffee, pepper and cardamom.

More on the [Coorg destination page](/destinations/coorg/).

## Chikmagalur at a glance

- **Mullayanagiri:** Karnataka's highest peak, at about 1,925 metres. The last stretch is on foot, up steps.
- **Baba Budangiri range:** grassland ridges and mist, next to Mullayanagiri.
- **Belur:** the Hoysala-era Chennakeshava temple, famous for its stone carvings.
- **Yagachi dam:** water sports near Belur.
- **Jhari Falls:** a waterfall reached by jeep track.

More on the [Chikmagalur destination page](/destinations/chikmagalur/).

## When to go

| Season | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| September to March | The main season: post-monsoon green, cooler mornings, clearer views (some mist at sunrise) |
| April and May | Warmer days; hills still cooler than the plains |
| June to August | Monsoon: full waterfalls, wet roads, misty viewpoints, plans can change |

HCC runs Coorg & Chikmagalur from Hyderabad every Thursday and Friday through the season.

## Getting there

### From Hyderabad

The practical route is an **overnight train to Mysore**, then road. On HCC's trip, the group leaves Kacheguda at 7 pm, freshens up and has breakfast in Mysore, and an AC vehicle covers everything from there, bringing the group back to Mysore for the 2:30 pm train home on day 3. You're back in Hyderabad at about 6 am the next day.

### From Bangalore

HCC's Bangalore departures go by Tempo Traveller from around KSR Railway Station / Majestic at 6 am on Fridays and Saturdays, with no train.

## How to combine them: a 3-day route

This is the route HCC uses from Hyderabad:

**Day 1: Mysore and Coorg.** Mysore Palace, then Abbey Falls and the Mandalpatti trek. Shopping for spices and coffee, and Raja's Seat if time permits.

**Day 2: Into Chikmagalur.** Dubare elephant camp, the carvings at Belur temple and water sports at Yagachi dam. The night is at a coffee plantation.

**Day 3: Mullayanagiri sunrise.** Up at 5 am for the peak, breakfast, then back to Mysore for lunch and the afternoon train.

The order can change with weather, traffic or local restrictions.

## Costs to plan for

On HCC's trip, the price includes return sleeper train tickets, two nights' stay (one at a coffee plantation), the AC vehicle and the trip captain. You pay separately for:

- **Food**
- **Entry tickets** (waterfalls, palace, temples, parks)
- **Jeep rides** (Mandalpatti and Mullayanagiri/Jhari are jeep country)
- **Water sports** at Yagachi, if you take them

## What to pack

- Light jacket or hoodie: hill mornings are cold, especially at Mullayanagiri
- Comfortable walking shoes with grip
- Rain gear in the monsoon
- Government photo ID (needed on trains)
- Power bank, water bottle, personal medicines

## Tips

- **Start early.** Sunrise points and popular falls are calmer early in the day.
- **Respect temple rules.** Dress modestly at Belur and the monastery.
- **Leave space for coffee.** Buying from estates or local shops is part of the experience.
- **Expect mist.** Views from Mandalpatti and Mullayanagiri aren't guaranteed; weather decides.
