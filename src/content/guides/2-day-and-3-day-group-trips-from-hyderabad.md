"Weekend trip" means different things depending on how far you go. A camp two hours away fits inside a Saturday and Sunday. A trip to Coorg or Gokarna needs an overnight train each way, which turns a 3-day itinerary into four nights away from home. This guide groups HCC's trips by how long they really take, so you can match a trip to the leave you have.

## The quick answer: days off needed

| Trip length | What it means | Days off (typical) | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 night / 2 days | Afternoon check-in, check out next morning | None for a Saturday camp | Ananthagiri Hills camps |
| 1 night / 2 days, transport included | Early-morning departure, back the next night | Often none (weekend dates) | Gandikota |
| 2 nights / 3 days + overnight train | Leave Thu or Fri evening, back Mon or Tue morning | 1 to 2 | Coorg, Gokarna, Ooty, Wayanad, Araku |
| 3 nights or more | Longer trips | 2 to 4 or more | Kerala, Hampi, Golden Triangle |

The days off assume a Monday-to-Friday job. Check the exact departure and return times on each trip page, because they vary.

## 2-day trips: one night out

### Ananthagiri Hills camps

The closest option. The campsites are near Vikarabad, about 80 km from Hyderabad, roughly 2 to 2.5 hours by road. You arrive in the afternoon, spend the evening at the camp and check out by 11 am. There's no group transport: you drive, or take the train to Vikarabad and an auto from the station.

### Gandikota

[Gandikota Canyon Camping](/trips/gandikota-camping-from-hyderabad/) is also one night, but much further. The vehicle leaves Hyderabad at 6 am on day 1 and you're back by night on day 2. It's a full two days, mostly on the road, in exchange for sunrise over the Pennar gorge.

## 3-day trips: two nights at the destination

These are the classic group weekend trips. The pattern on most of them:

1. **Day 0, evening:** board a non-AC sleeper train from Hyderabad (Kacheguda, Nampally or Secunderabad, depending on the trip).
2. **Days 1 to 3:** sightseeing by local vehicle, two nights' stay.
3. **Day 3, evening:** overnight train back.
4. **Day 4, morning:** arrive in Hyderabad.

### Picking between them by travel time

- **Shortest overnight train:** [Araku Valley, Vanajangi & Vizag](/trips/araku-vanajangi-vizag-from-hyderabad/) leaves on Thursday at 5:30 pm and the return train reaches Hyderabad at about 7 am.
- **Two batches a week:** [Coorg & Chikmagalur](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-hyderabad/), [Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-hyderabad/), [Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/) and [Isha, Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/isha-ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/) run Thursday and Friday batches. The Thursday batch is back on Monday and the Friday batch on Tuesday.
- **Two kinds of nights:** [Lonavala & Matheran](/trips/lonavala-matheran-from-hyderabad/) splits the stay between a homestay in Matheran and a lakeside camp at Pawna.

### What a 3-day group trip day looks like

Days are full. Expect early starts for viewpoints and sunrise spots (Mullayanagiri on the Coorg & Chikmagalur trip starts at 5 am, for example), long stretches in the vehicle between places, and free evenings at the stay. If you prefer a slower pace, pick a trip with fewer stops, such as [Wayanad](/trips/wayanad-from-hyderabad/) with two nights at one homestay.

## When 3 days isn't enough

If you can take most of a week, longer trips cover more ground without the rush:

- **[Kerala: Munnar & Alleppey](/trips/kerala-munnar-alleppey-from-hyderabad/)**: 3 nights, including a houseboat night.
- **[Hampi, Gokarna & Murudeshwar](/trips/hampi-gokarna-murudeshwar-from-hyderabad/)**: 3 nights by sleeper bus.
- **[Kerala: Munnar, Alleppey, Varkala & Trivandrum](/trips/kerala-backwaters-beaches-from-hyderabad/)**: 5 nights.

These usually run on dates given on request, so message HCC on WhatsApp for the next group.

## Tips for fitting a trip into your leave

- **Use long weekends.** A public holiday on a Friday or Monday turns a 2-night trip into one day of leave or none. See [long weekend trips from Hyderabad in 2027](/travel-guides/long-weekend-trips-from-hyderabad-2027/).
- **Sleep on the train.** The overnight trains are part of the plan; bring a light sheet and earplugs and you'll arrive ready for day 1.
- **Check the return time before you promise anyone a Monday meeting.** Return times are on each trip page, and for a few trips they're confirmed on WhatsApp.
