Packing for an organised camp is simpler than for wild camping: the tent, bedding and meals are provided. What you bring is about comfort, the weather and the activities. This checklist is written for one or two nights at a campsite like the ones near Hyderabad, and it works for camp nights on longer trips such as Gokarna and Dandeli too.

## The essentials

Don't leave without these:

- **Government photo ID**, original (needed at check-in and on trains). Some camps ask for an Aadhaar copy on WhatsApp when you book.
- **Phone and a charged power bank.** Charging points may not be available.
- **Torch or headlamp,** with spare batteries. Campsites are dark at night, and washrooms are shared.
- **Water bottle,** reusable.
- **Personal medicines,** plus anything you need for motion sickness on hill roads.
- **Some cash** for small purchases and nearby sights.

## Clothes

- One change of comfortable clothes per day
- Sleepwear
- **Light jacket or hoodie:** nights in the hills and the rocky canyon at Gandikota get cool
- **Full-sleeve top** for sun and mosquitoes
- Extra clothes for the pool or rain dance, if your camp has one
- Socks

### Footwear

- **Closed shoes with grip** (trekking or sports shoes) for treks, rope courses and rocky paths
- **Slippers** for the pool, washroom and around the tent

## Toiletries

Shared washrooms usually don't have supplies, so bring:

- Toothbrush and toothpaste
- Soap or body wash, shampoo
- Small towel (quick-dry is easiest)
- Sunscreen and lip balm
- Insect repellent
- Tissues or wet wipes
- Hand sanitiser
- Any personal hygiene items

## Activity extras

| If your camp has… | Add |
| --- | --- |
| A swimming pool | Swimwear, a towel, a plastic bag for wet clothes |
| A rain dance | A change of clothes and a bag for wet clothes |
| A sunrise trek | A warm layer and shoes with grip |
| A rope course | Closed shoes and comfortable clothes you can move in |
| River activities (Dandeli) | Swimwear, quick-dry towel, slippers with straps |

Check your camp page to see which activities are included. For example, the [budget Ananthagiri camp](/trips/ananthagiri-camping-from-hyderabad/) has a pool and rain dance, and [Trance Veechika](/trips/ananthagiri-trance-veechika-camping-from-hyderabad/) has a pool and a 6 am sunrise trek.

## Season extras

### Monsoon (June to September)

- Raincoat or poncho
- Quick-dry clothes
- Waterproof pouch for your phone
- An extra pair of socks

### Winter (November to February)

- A warmer jacket for night and early morning
- A cap or beanie

### Summer (March to May)

- Cap and sunglasses
- Extra sunscreen
- Electrolyte sachets

## A small day bag

Carry a small backpack for treks and sightseeing, with water, a snack (where allowed), sunscreen, your phone and a light layer. Leave the main bag at the tent.

## What to leave at home

- **Valuables** you don't need, such as jewellery or extra cards
- **Alcohol, hookah and anything illegal:** camp rules vary, and several campsites ban some or all of these. Breaking camp rules can mean removal without a refund.
- **Outside food** where the camp doesn't allow it (Sarpanpally, for example)
- **Speakers:** most camps have their own music sessions, and other campers want quiet
