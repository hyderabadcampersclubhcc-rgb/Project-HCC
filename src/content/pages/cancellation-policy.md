## The standard HCC policy

This applies to HCC's group trips unless the trip page says otherwise. Days are counted before the departure date.

| When you cancel | Cancellation fee |
|---|---|
| 30 or more days before departure | 25% of the trip price |
| 15 to 30 days before | 50% of the trip price |
| Less than 15 days before | No refund |

The booking amount (advance) is non-refundable in every case.

## Trips with their own rules

Some trips work differently. The rule shown on the trip page is the one that applies.

| Trip | Cancellation rule |
|---|---|
| [Pondicherry](/trips/pondicherry-from-hyderabad/) | Full refund up to 15 days before departure. 7–14 days: 50% fee. Less than 7 days: no refund. |
| [Ananthagiri Hills camping](/trips/ananthagiri-camping-from-hyderabad/) | The ₹500 per head advance is non-refundable. |
| [Gandikota camping](/trips/gandikota-camping-from-hyderabad/) | No refund once the booking is confirmed. If weather or government restrictions cancel the trip, you get a full refund or another date. |
| Sahyadri treks and Lonavala–Matheran | HCC's cancellation policy applies; train tickets follow IRCTC cancellation rules. |

## Train tickets

Where HCC books train tickets for the group, refunds on those tickets follow IRCTC rules and timelines.

## Missed departures

Please reach the boarding point on time. A missed train or bus can't be refunded.

## If the trip changes

- **Minimum group size:** if a departure doesn't reach the minimum group size, HCC may merge it with another batch or move you to the next date.
- **On the trip:** the sightseeing order can change with weather, traffic or local restrictions. Your trip captain decides on the day, for everyone's safety.
- **Stays:** if a listed stay is unavailable, a similar standard stay is arranged.

## How to cancel

Message HCC on WhatsApp or call [+91 81793 21382](tel:+918179321382) with your name, trip and departure date. The cancellation date is the day HCC receives your message.

Questions about a specific trip? Ask before you book. We'll confirm the exact terms for your date on WhatsApp.
