faqs:
  - q: How do I book a trip?
    a: Pick a trip and date on the website, then send an enquiry or message HCC on WhatsApp at +91 81793 21382. We confirm availability, you pay the advance to hold your seat, and you get the full trip details before departure.
  - q: How much is the advance, and when is the balance due?
    a: "{{advance-summary}}"
  - q: Can I join if I'm travelling alone?
    a: Yes. Solo travellers are welcome on HCC group trips. You're part of the group from the departure point, and the trip captain is with the group throughout.
  - q: Where do trips leave from?
    a: Most trips leave from Hyderabad by overnight train or bus, for example from Nampally or Kacheguda railway station. Several trips also run from Bangalore (from KSR Bengaluru for Gokarna). The boarding point and time for your city are on each trip page.
  - q: Which days do trips leave?
    a: Most weekend trips leave on Thursday or Friday evening and return early on Monday or Tuesday. Upcoming dates are on each trip page and on the upcoming trips page.
  - q: What is included in the price?
    a: It varies by trip. Every trip page lists what's included and what isn't, the transport class and timings, and the type of stay and room sharing, so you can check before you pay.
  - q: What is the cancellation policy?
    a: "{{cancellation-summary}} See the cancellation policy page for the full policy."
  - q: What if my trip doesn't fill up?
    a: "{{hcc-cancellation}}"
  - q: Can the plan change during the trip?
    a: Yes, the sightseeing order can change with weather, traffic or local restrictions. Your trip captain decides on the day, for everyone's safety.
  - q: Are these luxury tours?
    a: No. Most HCC trips are budget group trips, comfortable and well run, with sharing stays. Trips generally use shared stays. Separate couples trips are available with private rooms.
  - q: Can children come?
    a: On many trips, yes. Child rates differ by trip, and some treks have an age range, so ask HCC when you enquire.
  - q: Can HCC plan a private trip for our group?
    a: Yes. HCC plans private and customised versions of its trips for friends, families and teams. Send your dates, group size and destination through the customised trips page or on WhatsApp.
