## Who we are

This website is run by Hyderabad Campers Club ("HCC", "we"), based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. If you have any question about your data, contact us using the details on our [contact page](/contact/).

## What we collect

When you send an enquiry, request an itinerary or ask to book a seat, we collect:

- your **name** and **mobile/WhatsApp number** (required)
- optional details you choose to share: destination, travel dates, number of travellers, budget and travel type
- the trip, date and options you selected, and the price quoted
- how you found us: the page you landed on and campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source, or an ad click ID such as gclid or fbclid)

We do **not** collect payment card details on this website.

## How we use it

- to reply to your enquiry and send trip details, usually on WhatsApp or by phone
- to confirm and manage your booking
- to understand which pages and advertising campaigns bring enquiries, so we can improve them

We do not sell your personal information.

## Where it is stored

Enquiries are stored in a Google Sheet on HCC's Google account, with a private backup copy on our web hosting provider's server (Hostinger). Only the HCC team can access them.

## Cookies and analytics

This website stores small pieces of information in your browser that it needs to work: the campaign link you arrived from, your "compare trips" selection and your cookie choice.

Analytics and advertising tools are only used if you allow them in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time from **Privacy choices** at the bottom of every page.

- **Analytics** (Google Analytics): which pages and trips are viewed and which buttons are used. We never send your name or phone number to Google.
- **Advertising** (Google Ads and Meta, the company behind Instagram and Facebook): which ads lead to enquiries, and showing our trips to people who visited this website. If you allow Advertising and send an enquiry, our server also tells Meta that an enquiry was made, together with your phone number in a scrambled (hashed) form that Meta uses only to match the enquiry to an ad. Your name and message are never shared.

Booking, enquiries and WhatsApp work the same whatever you choose.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and accounting requirements, and then delete them.

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
