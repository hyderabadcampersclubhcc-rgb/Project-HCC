Hyderabad Campers Club (HCC) runs group trips for people who want to get out of the city with a group: weekend getaways, Sahyadri treks, camping nights and longer trips across India. Trips leave from Hyderabad, and several also run from Bangalore.

## What we run

- **[Weekend trips from Hyderabad](/weekend-trips-from-hyderabad/):** Coorg, Chikmagalur, Gokarna, Ooty, Wayanad, Munnar, Hampi, Pondicherry, Araku and more, mostly leaving on a Thursday or Friday evening.
- **[Camping near Hyderabad](/camping-near-hyderabad/):** four campsites in the Ananthagiri Hills.
- **Treks:** monsoon treks in the Sahyadris, including Andharban, Devkund and Harishchandragad.
- **Longer trips:** Kerala, Goa, Manali, Kashmir and the Golden Triangle.
- **Couples trips** and **[customised trips](/customised-trips/)** for friends, families and teams.

## How HCC trips work

- **Everything is on the trip page.** Each trip lists the day-by-day plan, the transport (train or bus, with the class and timings), the type of stay and room sharing, and what's included and what isn't.
- **A trip captain is with the group.** On HCC group trips (camping stays and couples packages work differently), the trip captain travels with the group and makes the on-the-day calls (sightseeing order, weather changes) with everyone's safety in mind.
- **Solo travellers are welcome.** You join the group from the departure point.
- **Budget group travel.** Most trips are comfortable, well-run budget trips with sharing stays, not luxury tours.
- **Clear policies.** The advance, the balance and the [cancellation policy](/cancellation-policy/) are stated before you pay.
