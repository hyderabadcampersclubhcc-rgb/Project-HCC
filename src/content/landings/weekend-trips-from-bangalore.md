## Weekend trips from Bangalore with HCC

HCC's Bangalore departures cover three very different weekends: coffee estates and peaks, a beach-and-river camping trip, and Kerala's tea country. All are 2 nights / 3 days and leave at the end of the working week.

### Coorg & Chikmagalur

The **[Coorg & Chikmagalur trip from Bangalore](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-bangalore/)** runs every Friday. The group meets at 6 am on Saturday for breakfast, then visits the Golden Temple (Namdroling Monastery), the elephant camp and Raja's Seat. Day 2 starts at 5 am with a jeep ride to Mandalpatti and Abbey Falls, then on to Chikmagalur via the Belur temple and water sports at Yagachi dam. Day 3 is Mullayanagiri, Karnataka's highest peak, and Jhari Falls before heading home. From ₹6,999, with stays, local transport and a trip guide; food, entry tickets and jeep rides are extra. More about [Coorg](/destinations/coorg/).

### Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar

The **[Gokarna trip from Bangalore](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-bangalore/)** leaves **KSR Bengaluru at 11 pm on Friday** on the overnight train to Hubballi. From there it's Vibhuthi falls, the Murudeshwar temple, a night at a beach camp, the Mahabaleshwar Temple and Om Beach, then a riverside camp in Dandeli with kayaking, zorbing and river boating. From ₹5,999, including the sleeper train, two camp nights and three meals. There's also a [couples version](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-couple-from-bangalore/). More about [Gokarna](/destinations/gokarna/).

### Munnar

The **[Munnar trip from Bangalore](/trips/munnar-from-bangalore/)** goes by train to Coimbatore, then up through the Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary for two days of tea estates, Top Station, Mattupetty Dam and Eravikulam National Park. It includes 3-star stays and daily breakfast. From ₹8,999. More about [Munnar](/destinations/munnar/).

## Longer trips from Bangalore

For a 4-day break, **[Kerala: Munnar & Alleppey](/trips/kerala-munnar-alleppey-from-bangalore/)** adds Athirappilly falls and a night on an Alleppey houseboat (from ₹12,999). See the [Kerala backwaters](/destinations/alleppey/).

## How to choose

- **Beaches and camping:** Gokarna–Dandeli, the lowest-priced weekend from Bangalore.
- **Hills, coffee and a sunrise peak:** Coorg & Chikmagalur.
- **Tea country with more comfort:** Munnar, with 3-star stays and breakfast.

## Before you book

- These are budget group trips: comfortable and well organised, not luxury tours.
- Carry a government photo ID for train travel.
- Plans can change with the weather; the trip captain decides on the day.
- Message HCC on WhatsApp to confirm the next Bangalore departure before you pay.
