## Which camp suits you?

- **Lowest price, pool and music:** [Budget Camping in Ananthagiri Hills with Pool & Music](/trips/ananthagiri-camping-from-hyderabad/), with a pool from 5 to 10 pm and a Saturday DJ night.
- **Keep busy all afternoon:** [Premium Adventure Camping in Ananthagiri Hills](/trips/ananthagiri-harivillu-camping-from-hyderabad/) at Harivillu Resort, with a rope course, cycling and a movie under the stars.
- **Wake up by the water:** [Lakeside Camping Near Hyderabad](/trips/ananthagiri-sarpanpally-lake-camping-from-hyderabad/) at Sarpanpally Lake, with lakeside tents, a bonfire and a farm visit.
- **A little more comfort:** [Premium Camping in Ananthagiri Hills](/trips/ananthagiri-trance-veechika-camping-from-hyderabad/) at Trance Veechika Resort, with a pool and a guided 6 am sunrise trek.

## Getting there from Hyderabad

Ananthagiri Hills is near Vikarabad, about 80 km west of Hyderabad, roughly 2 to 2.5 hours by road via the Chevella road. Without a car, take a train to Vikarabad (for example the 12702 from Nampally at 2:55 pm) or a TSRTC bus, then an auto or cab to your campsite. Exact directions come with your booking.

For more camps around the city, see [camping near Hyderabad](/camping-near-hyderabad/). For the hills themselves (viewpoints, the temple and the best time to go), read the [Ananthagiri Hills travel guide](/destinations/ananthagiri/).

## Good to know

- Carry a government photo ID for every guest.
- Activities depend on the weather.
- Transport isn't included at any of the four camps.
