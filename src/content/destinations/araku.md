## Why visit Araku Valley

Araku is the Eastern Ghats at their best: green hills, coffee estates, waterfalls and the Borra Caves, a short drive up from the Vizag coast. Nearby Vanajangi has become famous for one thing: on clear winter mornings, the sun rises over a sea of clouds.

## Getting to Araku from Hyderabad

HCC's **[Araku Valley, Vanajangi & Vizag trip](/trips/araku-vanajangi-vizag-from-hyderabad/)** leaves **Kacheguda at 5:30 pm on Thursday** and reaches **Vizag at 7 am**. Day 1 heads up to Araku via **Borra Caves**, Katiki falls and the Tribal and Coffee Museums. Day 2 covers Madagada viewpoint, Padmapuram Gardens, Galikonda, the wooden bridge, the pine forest and Chaparai falls, then on to Vanajangi for a bonfire. Day 3 starts with the **Vanajangi sunrise trek**, then Kailasagiri and Vizag beach before the **8 pm train**, arriving in Hyderabad at 7 am.

## The HCC Araku trip

**Araku Valley, Vanajangi & Vizag** (2 nights / 3 days, from ₹6,999): non-AC sleeper trains, a tempo traveller for three days, stays on 3/4 sharing, a bonfire with music and a trip coordinator. Every Thursday from Hyderabad.

## Who the Araku trip suits

The trip is built for people who want to join a group: solo travellers, friends planning a weekend, and anyone who enjoys nature and a little trekking. The pace is full (caves, waterfalls and viewpoints on day 1 and 2, and an early start for the sunrise on day 3), with a bonfire and music in the evening at Vanajangi. You don't need to come with a group: you're welcome to join solo and travel with the others from Kacheguda.

## Best time to visit

September to February is the best time, especially for the Vanajangi cloud sunrise, which needs cool, clear mornings. The monsoon makes Araku very green but the sunrise less certain.

## Good to know

- The sunrise trek is short but early: carry a torch and a warm layer.
- Food, entry fees and jeep rides are paid locally.
