## Why visit Ananthagiri Hills

Ananthagiri Hills near Vikarabad is one of the closest forested hill areas to Hyderabad: close enough for an overnight escape without taking leave. HCC's camp turns it into an easy, social night out: tents with proper bedding, a swimming pool, games, music and a hot local buffet, with the forest and nearby viewpoints around you.

## The HCC camp

The **[Ananthagiri Hills Camping with Pool & Music Night](/trips/ananthagiri-camping-from-hyderabad/)** runs from a **4 pm check-in to an 11 am check-out**:

- **Evening:** tea and camp snacks 5–6 pm; the pool open 5–10 pm; indoor and outdoor games until 11:50 pm.
- **Night:** a Saturday DJ session from 8 pm, then the dinner buffet 8:30–10 pm (bagara rice, steamed rice, chicken curry, veg curry, sambar). The campsite gate closes at 10 pm.
- **Morning:** breakfast at 8:30 am (puri with curry, upma, idli or bonda, with tea and coffee), then check-out by 11 am.

## Getting there from Hyderabad

- **Self-drive** by car or bike; parking is free, at the owner's risk.
- **By train:** the 12702 from Nampally at 2:55 pm (also stopping at Begumpet and Lingampalli) reaches Vikarabad at 4:02 pm; the 12732 from Secunderabad at 4:00 pm reaches at 5:15 pm.
- **From Vikarabad station** the campsite is about 9 km; autos and cabs are available. Carpooling may be possible depending on group size.

## Booking and price

₹1,600 per person, with a ₹500 per head advance to confirm (the balance is paid at check-in). Call or WhatsApp to check availability first: walk-ins aren't allowed. The advance is non-refundable.

## Good to know

- Bring an extra pair of clothes for the pool, a towel and toiletries, and a hoodie for the night.
- Other-state alcohol and hookah aren't allowed in the campsite.
- The trekking point is about 4 km from the campsite; transport there is on your own.
