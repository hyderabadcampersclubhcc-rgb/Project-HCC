## Why visit Ananthagiri Hills

Ananthagiri Hills near Vikarabad is one of the closest forested hill areas to Hyderabad: close enough for an overnight escape without taking leave. Forest trails, viewpoints and nearby lakes make it a natural place to camp for a night.

## HCC's campsites at Ananthagiri

HCC offers four overnight camps here, each with one price per person:

- **[Budget-Friendly Camping Near Ananthagiri Hills](/trips/ananthagiri-camping-from-hyderabad/)**: tents with bedding, a pool from 5 to 10 pm, games, a Saturday DJ night, a campfire and a hot buffet. Check in at 4 pm, check out at 11 am. From ₹1,600.
- **[Harivillu Resort](/trips/ananthagiri-harivillu-camping-from-hyderabad/)**: a rope adventure course, cycling, a short trek, sports, a bonfire and a movie night. From ₹2,000.
- **[Sarpanpally Lake](/trips/ananthagiri-sarpanpally-lake-camping-from-hyderabad/)**: lakeside tents, a bonfire hour, games and live-kitchen meals. From ₹2,000.
- **[Trance Veechika](/trips/ananthagiri-trance-veechika-camping-from-hyderabad/)**: premium camping with a pool, rain dance and a guided sunrise trek. From ₹2,999.

To see them side by side, [compare the Ananthagiri campsites](/camping-near-hyderabad/ananthagiri-hills/). For other ideas, see [camping near Hyderabad](/camping-near-hyderabad/).

## Getting there from Hyderabad

- **Self-drive:** about 80 km by car or bike, roughly 2 to 2.5 hours.
- **By train:** the 12702 from Nampally at 2:55 pm (also stopping at Begumpet and Lingampalli) reaches Vikarabad at 4:02 pm. The 12732 from Secunderabad at 4:00 pm reaches at 5:15 pm.
- **From Vikarabad station:** autos and cabs go to the campsites. Exact directions come with your booking.

## When to go

July to February is the best time: the hills are green after the monsoon and the nights are cooler. Check dates for your campsite with HCC on WhatsApp.

## Good to know

- Bring a hoodie for the night, a torch, toiletries and an extra set of clothes if your camp has a pool.
- Carry a government photo ID for every guest.
- Nearby, on your own transport: Kotpalli Lake (boating and kayaking), the Anantha Padmanabha Swamy Temple, the Ananthagiri viewpoint and the Damagundam Shiva Temple.
