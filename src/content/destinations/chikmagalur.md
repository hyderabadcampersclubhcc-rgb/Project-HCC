## Why visit Chikmagalur

Chikmagalur is the quieter coffee district north of Coorg: grassy ridges, the Baba Budangiri range and Mullayanagiri, Karnataka's highest peak. It also holds some of the finest Hoysala temple carving at Belur. For a weekend from Hyderabad or Bangalore it fits best alongside Coorg, which is exactly how HCC runs it.

## How HCC's trip covers Chikmagalur

On the **[Coorg & Chikmagalur trip](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-hyderabad/)**, day 1 is in Coorg. Day 2 drives north via the Dubare elephant camp to **Belur temple** and **Yagachi dam** for water sports, with the night in Chikmagalur. Day 3 starts at **5 am for Mullayanagiri**, then breakfast and the drive back to Mysore for the 2:30 pm train.

## Getting there

- **From Hyderabad:** the overnight train from Kacheguda at 7 pm on Friday to Mysore, then by road. Back in Hyderabad around 5:30 am after day 3.
- **From Bangalore:** the same trip runs every Friday from Bangalore; the group meets at 6 am on Saturday. See [Coorg & Chikmagalur from Bangalore](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-bangalore/).

## Best time to visit

September to March gives the clearest views from Mullayanagiri and pleasant walking weather. From June to September the hills are lush and misty but wet; paths up the peak can be slippery.

## Good to know

- The trip is 2 nights / 3 days from ₹6,999, with sleeper-class train tickets, an AC vehicle and sharing stays.
- Food, entry tickets and jeep rides are paid locally.
- The Mullayanagiri morning is early and cool: carry a light jacket and good shoes.
