## Why visit Pondicherry

Pondicherry combines a seaside promenade, the pastel streets of the French Quarter, café culture and Auroville's quiet township. Down the coast, the Pichavaram mangroves are one of the largest in the world, best seen by boat. It's a relaxed, walkable weekend rather than an adventure trip.

## Getting to Pondicherry from Hyderabad

HCC's **[Pondicherry trip](/trips/pondicherry-from-hyderabad/)** departs from **Nampally station at 5 pm**, with a pre-hired tempo for local travel. The trip covers **Auroville**, the **French Colony**, **Rock Beach and Promenade Beach** (with sunrise and sunset on the coast) and a boat ride through the **Pichavaram mangroves**.

## Which Pondicherry trip to choose

- **[Pondicherry](/trips/pondicherry-from-hyderabad/)** (2 nights / 3 days, from ₹7,199): the French Quarter, Auroville, the beaches and Pichavaram.
- **[Arunachalam, Pondicherry & Mahabalipuram](/trips/arunachalam-pondicherry-from-hyderabad/)** (2 nights / 3 days, from ₹7,499): day 1 at Tiruvannamalai (Sri Ramana Maharshi Ashram and the Arunachaleswara Temple aarti, with an optional 14 km Giri Pradakshinam at night), day 2 Pichavaram and Pondicherry, day 3 Mahabalipuram's shore temples. AC stays and an AC vehicle; by sleeper train or semi-sleeper bus.

## Best time to visit

October to February is the most pleasant time for sightseeing and the beaches. March to June is warm and humid. July to September brings heavy rain, which can limit some outdoor plans.

## Good to know

- Food isn't included; Pondicherry has plenty of cafés.
- Children are welcome; ask HCC for the child price.
- Freshen-up rooms aren't provided on long weekends.
- Cancellation: full refund up to 15 days before, 50% from 7 to 14 days, no refund within 7 days.
