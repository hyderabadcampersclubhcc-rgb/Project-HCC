## Why visit Munnar

Munnar is Kerala's tea capital: hills covered edge to edge in tea gardens, cool mornings, viewpoints like Top Station and Echo Point, and Eravikulam National Park, home of the Nilgiri tahr. HCC's Munnar trip is also one of the more comfortable weekends, with 3-star stays and breakfast every day.

## Getting to Munnar from Hyderabad and Bangalore

HCC's **[Munnar trip](/trips/munnar-from-hyderabad/)** goes by train to **Coimbatore, arriving at 8 am**, and runs from **Hyderabad, Bangalore and Vijayawada**. Day 1 drives up through the **Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary** to Lakkom falls, the botanical garden and a zipline and swing. Day 2 starts at 5 am for **Top Station and Echo Point**, then Mattupetty Dam, a tea factory and rose garden, the elephant camp and a spice plantation. Day 3 covers Gap Road, Idli Hills and **Eravikulam National Park** before the drive back to Coimbatore for the train.

From Bangalore, see [Munnar from Bangalore](/trips/munnar-from-bangalore/).

## Which Munnar trip to choose

- **[Munnar](/trips/munnar-from-hyderabad/)** (2 nights / 3 days, from ₹8,999): the focused tea-country weekend, with 3-star stays and daily breakfast.
- **[Kerala: Munnar & Alleppey](/trips/kerala-munnar-alleppey-from-hyderabad/)** (3 nights / 4 days, from ₹12,999): Athirappilly falls, two days in Munnar and a night on an Alleppey houseboat. From Hyderabad or [Bangalore](/trips/kerala-munnar-alleppey-from-bangalore/).
- **[Kerala: Munnar, Alleppey, Varkala & Trivandrum](/trips/kerala-backwaters-beaches-from-hyderabad/)** (5 nights / 6 days, from ₹13,999): the full Kerala circuit.

## Best time to visit

September to May is the best time, with clear viewpoints and cool weather. Munnar is especially green just after the monsoon. June to August brings heavy rain and mist.

## Good to know

- Meals other than breakfast, entry tickets, activities and jeep rides are paid locally.
- Mornings are cold at Top Station: carry a warm layer.
