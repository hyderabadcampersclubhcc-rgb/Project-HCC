## Why visit Alleppey

Alleppey (Alappuzha) is the heart of Kerala's backwaters: a maze of palm-lined canals, lakes and villages best seen from a houseboat. A night on board, with meals cooked on the boat and a slow cruise at sunset, is the classic Kerala experience.

## HCC trips with an Alleppey houseboat

- **[Kerala: Munnar & Alleppey](/trips/kerala-munnar-alleppey-from-hyderabad/)** (3 nights / 4 days, from ₹12,999): by sleeper train to **Kochi** (arriving around noon), Athirappilly falls, two days in Munnar, then Marari Beach and an **Alleppey houseboat**: check in at 2 pm for lunch and a cruise, dinner on board, and a one-hour morning cruise. From Hyderabad or **[Bangalore](/trips/kerala-munnar-alleppey-from-bangalore/)**.
- **[Kerala: Munnar, Alleppey, Varkala & Trivandrum](/trips/kerala-backwaters-beaches-from-hyderabad/)** (5 nights / 6 days, from ₹13,999): adds a Kathakali evening, Eravikulam, Jatayu Earth Centre, the **Varkala cliff** and Trivandrum's temples, with AC transport throughout.
- **[Kerala Honeymoon](/trips/kerala-honeymoon-from-kochi/)** (₹45,000 per couple, from Kochi): Athirappilly, Munnar and a **private houseboat** night.

## Getting there

From Hyderabad or Bangalore, HCC's Kerala trips go by **overnight sleeper train to Kochi**. Alleppey comes after the Munnar days, so the trip ends close to Kochi for the train home (or continues south to Varkala and Trivandrum on the 6-day trip).

## Best time to visit

September to March is the best time for the backwaters, with pleasant weather after the monsoon. April and May are hot; June to August is the heavy monsoon.

## Good to know

- On the 4-day trip, daily breakfast is included, plus lunch, dinner and breakfast on the houseboat; other meals are extra.
- Boating, jeep safaris and elephant rides are paid separately.
