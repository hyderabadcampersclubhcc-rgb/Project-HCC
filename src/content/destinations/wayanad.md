## Why visit Wayanad

Wayanad is Kerala's green highland district: forested hills, tea and spice estates, river islands and the short trek up Chembra Peak to its heart-shaped lake. It feels very different from the coast and makes an easy long weekend from Hyderabad by train via Mysore.

## Getting to Wayanad from Hyderabad

HCC's **[Wayanad trip](/trips/wayanad-from-hyderabad/)** leaves **Kacheguda at 7 pm on Thursday** and reaches **Mysore by 9 am**, where freshen-up rooms are provided. Day 1 drives to Wayanad via Kuruva Dweep, Banasura Sagar dam and the Lakkidi viewpoint, with a zipline before the homestay. Day 2 starts early (ready by 4:30 am) for the **Chembra Peak trek**, then the **900 Kandi glass bridge**, Soochipara falls if time permits, and shopping. On day 3 the group visits Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary, and Mysore Palace if time permits, before the train back around 3 pm.

## Which Wayanad trip to choose

- **[Wayanad](/trips/wayanad-from-hyderabad/)** (2 nights / 3 days, from ₹7,999): Chembra, the glass bridge, Banasura and the wildlife sanctuary. Every Thursday from Hyderabad.
- **[Wayanad, Ooty & Mysore](/trips/wayanad-ooty-mysore-from-hyderabad/)** (4 nights / 5 days, from ₹12,999): two days in Wayanad (with a swimming pool at the stay), a drive through Mudumalai Tiger Reserve, then Ooty and Coonoor with the toy train included.

## Best time to visit

October to May is the best time for the Chembra trek and viewpoints. The monsoon (June to September) brings heavy rain; the forests are at their greenest, but treks can be closed or slippery.

## Good to know

- HCC's Wayanad trip is rated moderate because of the early Chembra trek; the rest is easy sightseeing.
- Stays are in a homestay on a sharing basis, with sleeper-class trains both ways.
- Food, entry tickets and jeep rides are paid locally.
