## Why visit Hampi

Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara empire, and its ruins are spread across a landscape of giant granite boulders along the Tungabhadra river. It's one of India's most remarkable heritage sites, best explored slowly on foot, by auto and by coracle, and it's an overnight bus ride from Hyderabad.

## Getting to Hampi from Hyderabad

HCC's **[Hampi, Gokarna & Murudeshwar trip](/trips/hampi-gokarna-murudeshwar-from-hyderabad/)** leaves Hyderabad by **sleeper bus at 9 pm** and reaches **Hospet at 6 am**. Day 1 explores the Virupaksha temple, Lotus Mahal and the ruins, then a **coracle ride on the Tungabhadra**, sunset at **Hemakuta Hill** and the Hippie Market. Day 2 covers the Ganesha temples and the **Vitthala temple** with its stone chariot and musical pillars, before an overnight drive to the coast for Murudeshwar and Gokarna. The bus is back in Hyderabad around 11 am.

## The HCC Hampi trip

**Hampi, Gokarna & Murudeshwar** (3 nights / 4 days, from ₹8,999): two days of Hampi with a guided tour and the coracle ride, rooms in Hampi and camping in Gokarna, entry fees included. Food and 5% GST are extra.

## Best time to visit

October to February is the best time, with warm days and cool evenings. From March to May it gets very hot for walking among the ruins; the monsoon is quieter, and the river is fuller.

## Good to know

- Wear clothing suitable for temple visits and comfortable shoes for the boulders.
- Carry a 2-litre water bottle: the sites are spread out and exposed.
- Honnavar boating on the way to the coast is optional and paid separately.

Continue to the coast: [Gokarna](/destinations/gokarna/).
