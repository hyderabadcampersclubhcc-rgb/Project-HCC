## Why visit Ooty

Ooty is a classic first hill-station weekend from Hyderabad: tea slopes, pine forests, a lake, cool evenings and the toy train down to Coonoor. It's an easy place to enjoy as a group. Most sights are short drives apart, and the Ooty–Coonoor stretch fits comfortably into two days.

## Getting to Ooty from Hyderabad

HCC's Ooty trips are built around the overnight **Mysore Express from Kacheguda at 7 pm**. You reach Mysore by about 10 am, freshen up, and see Mysore Palace before the drive up to Ooty through **Bandipur forest**, stopping at Needle Rock viewpoint, Pykara falls and Shooting Point on the way. The return is the same route: the drive back down to Mysore on day 3 and the overnight train home.

Sleeper class is standard; some departures offer a 3AC upgrade (+₹1,400).

## From Bangalore

HCC doesn't currently run a fixed Ooty departure from Bangalore. If you're in Bangalore and want to travel as a group, [ask HCC for a custom plan](/customised-trips/).

## Which HCC Ooty trip is right for you?

- **[Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/)** (2 nights / 3 days, from ₹7,999): the classic weekend. Mysore Palace, Pykara, the toy train, Dolphin's Nose, a tea factory and the Pine Forest. Leaves every Friday.
- **[Isha, Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/isha-ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/)** (2 nights / 3 days, from ₹7,999): the same hills plus the Adiyogi light show at the Isha Yoga Center.
- **[Wayanad, Ooty & Mysore](/trips/wayanad-ooty-mysore-from-hyderabad/)** (4 nights / 5 days, from ₹12,999): two days in Wayanad, a drive through Mudumalai, then Ooty and Coonoor with the toy train ticket included.
- **[Ooty & Coonoor for couples](/trips/ooty-coonoor-couple-from-hyderabad/)** (₹37,000 per couple): the same itinerary with a private couple room.

## Best time to visit

Ooty is pleasant for most of the year. From October to June the weather is generally good for sightseeing, with cool evenings. July to September brings heavy monsoon rain to the Nilgiris: greener, but expect wet days and some changes to plans. Pack a warm layer whatever the month.

## Good to know

- Stays on HCC's Ooty trips are on a sharing basis (3–4 per room), with couple rooms available at extra cost.
- Food and entry tickets (Mysore Palace, Pykara, the toy train) are paid locally unless the trip lists them as included.
- Carry a government photo ID for the train.
