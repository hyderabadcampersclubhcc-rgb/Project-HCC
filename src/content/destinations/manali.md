## Why visit Manali

Manali is the classic Himalayan escape: deodar forests, the wooden Hadimba Temple, the cafés of Old Manali, and snow within reach at Solang Valley and through the Atal Tunnel at Sissu. Down the valley, Kasol and Manikaran in the Parvati valley, and Shimla to the south, make natural add-ons.

## How HCC's Manali trips work

Both trips start in **Delhi** with an overnight **Volvo semi-sleeper** (leaving between 6 pm and 11 pm, about 12 hours) and return the same way. Travellers from Hyderabad or Bangalore reach Delhi first.

- **[Manali & Kasol](/trips/manali-kasol-from-delhi/)** (3 nights / 4 days, from ₹12,000): Hadimba Temple, Old Manali and Mall Road; Solang Valley and the Atal Tunnel to Sissu; Kullu (rafting and paragliding optional) and a **riverside camp night in Kasol** with a bonfire and music; then the Chalal walk and Manikaran's hot springs. 3 breakfasts and 3 dinners.
- **[Shimla & Manali](/trips/shimla-manali-from-delhi/)** (4 nights / 5 days, from ₹11,999 on triple sharing, ₹14,000 on double): Manali's sights, Solang and Sissu, then via Kullu to **Shimla** for Kufri, the Ridge, Christ Church, Lakkar Bazaar and Jakhu Temple. 4 breakfasts and 4 dinners.

## Best time to visit

March to June is pleasant for sightseeing, with snow still around Solang and Sissu early in the season. October to February brings colder weather and snowfall: beautiful, but roads can close and snow-vehicle charges may apply. The monsoon months see landslide risk on mountain roads.

## Good to know

- Carry warm layers, thermals, gloves and waterproof shoes.
- Paragliding, rafting and entry fees are paid separately.
- Heavy snow can close roads; plans change on the day for safety.

Also on the trip: Kasol and Shimla.
