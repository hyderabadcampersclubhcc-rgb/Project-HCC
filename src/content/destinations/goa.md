## Why visit Goa

Goa needs little introduction: beaches, forts, water sports, Portuguese-era churches and a laid-back evening scene. Done as a group from Hyderabad with a beachside resort, it's one of the easiest fun weekends.

## Getting to Goa from Hyderabad

HCC's **[Goa trip](/trips/goa-from-hyderabad/)** goes by **non-AC sleeper train**, arriving at **9 am**. An AC tempo takes the group to a **beachside resort** (AC rooms, a pool) and covers all the sightseeing:

- **Day 1, North Goa:** Vagator Beach, Chapora Fort, Fort Aguada, Baga and Calangute, then a pool party at the resort.
- **Day 2, water sports:** jet ski, banana and bumper rides, speed boat and parasailing (when operating; paid on the spot), then a relaxed evening.
- **Day 3, Old Goa:** early photos on Parra Road, then Sinquerim Fort, the Old Goa church, Goa Museum, the street market and Anjuna Beach before the evening train home.

## Price and inclusions

From **₹7,999 per person** for 2 nights / 3 days: return train tickets, two nights in an AC room at a beachside resort, local transport for three days with pickup and drop for water sports, and tolls, parking and permits. Water activities, food and shopping are extra. 50% of the package cost is paid at confirmation.

## Best time to visit

November to March is the best time for the beaches and water sports, with calmer seas. The monsoon (June to September) brings rough seas, and water sports are often suspended.

## Good to know

- Carry swimwear, sunscreen and a cap.
- If the listed resort is unavailable, a similar standard stay is arranged.
