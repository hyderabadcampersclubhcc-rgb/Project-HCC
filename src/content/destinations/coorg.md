## Why visit Coorg

Coorg (Kodagu) is Karnataka's coffee country: misty hills, waterfalls, plantation stays and a strong local culture. It works well as a long weekend because it pairs naturally with Chikmagalur, the other coffee district a few hours north. HCC's trips combine the two.

## Getting to Coorg from Hyderabad

HCC's **Coorg & Chikmagalur** trip leaves **Kacheguda at 7 pm every Thursday and Friday** on the overnight train to **Mysore**. A freshen-up room and breakfast in Mysore come first, then Mysore Palace, Abbey Falls, the Mandalpatti trek and Raja's Seat, with the first night at a coffee plantation. Day 2 moves to Chikmagalur via the Dubare elephant camp, Belur temple and water sports at Yagachi dam. On day 3 the group climbs Mullayanagiri at 5 am, heads back to Mysore for lunch, and catches the 2:30 pm train, reaching Hyderabad around 6:00 am on Monday (Thursday batch) or Tuesday (Friday batch).

## From Bangalore

The same trip runs **every Friday and Saturday from Bangalore** (from ₹6,999), entirely by Tempo Traveller with no train. Pickup is around KSR Railway Station (SBC) / Majestic Bus Stand at 6 am; the Friday batch is back on Sunday and the Saturday batch on Monday around 6 pm. See [Coorg & Chikmagalur from Bangalore](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-bangalore/).

## Which Coorg trip to choose

- **[Coorg & Chikmagalur from Hyderabad](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-hyderabad/)** (2 nights / 3 days + overnight train travel, from ₹6,999): both coffee districts, a plantation night and Mullayanagiri. Every Friday.
- **[Coorg from Mysore](/trips/coorg-from-mysore/)** (2 nights / 3 days, from ₹6,499): for anyone already in Mysore or Bangalore. The Golden Temple, Nisargadhama, Dubare, Mandalpatti with a zipline, Abbey Falls, Madikeri Fort and a coffee-plantation walk.

## Best time to visit

Coorg is good for most of the year. October to March is cool and dry, ideal for viewpoints and treks. June to September is the monsoon: the waterfalls are at their fullest and everything is green, but expect rain and slippery paths.

## Good to know

- Stays are on a sharing basis; the Coorg & Chikmagalur trip includes sleeper-class train tickets and an AC vehicle.
- Food, entry tickets and jeep rides are paid locally.
- Bring rain gear in the monsoon and shoes with grip for Mandalpatti and Mullayanagiri.
