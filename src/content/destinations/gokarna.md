## Why visit Gokarna

Gokarna is two places in one: an old temple town built around the Mahabaleshwar Temple, and a string of beaches and headlands along the coast just outside it, Om Beach the most famous. It's quieter than Goa, and pairs perfectly with the forests and river camps of Dandeli, a few hours inland.

## Getting to Gokarna from Hyderabad

HCC's Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar trip leaves **Nampally station at 3 pm every Thursday and Friday** on the overnight train to **Hubballi**, arriving about 6:30 am. From Hubballi a private vehicle takes the group to Vibhuthi falls for a short trek and a swim, then to Murudeshwar for the evening and a night camping at Nirvana Beach. Day 2 starts at the Mahabaleshwar Temple at 6 am, then Mirjan Fort and Om Beach, before the drive to Dandeli. The return train leaves Hubballi at 9:05 pm on day 3 and reaches Hyderabad around 10 am on Monday (Thursday batch) or Tuesday (Friday batch).

## From Bangalore

The same trip runs from Bangalore: the group leaves **KSR Bengaluru at 11 pm on Friday** for Hubballi and joins the same itinerary. See **[Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar from Bangalore](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-bangalore/)**. There's also a [departure from Vijayawada](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-vijayawada/) at 2 pm.

## Which Gokarna trip to choose

- **[Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-hyderabad/)** (2 nights / 3 days + overnight train travel, from ₹5,999): beach camp, river camp, Vibhuthi falls, the Murudeshwar temple, Om Beach and Kali river activities, with one breakfast, one lunch and one dinner. Every Friday.
- **[Hampi, Gokarna & Murudeshwar](/trips/hampi-gokarna-murudeshwar-from-hyderabad/)** (3 nights / 4 days, from ₹8,999): a full day in [Hampi's ruins](/destinations/hampi/) with a coracle ride, then Murudeshwar and Gokarna, by sleeper bus.
- **[Gokarna & Dandeli for couples](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-couple-from-hyderabad/)** (₹37,000 per couple): the beach-and-river weekend with a couple tent.

## Best time to visit

October to March is the best time for Gokarna's beaches, with calmer seas and pleasant weather. The monsoon (June to September) makes the sea rough and some beach stops may be skipped, though Dandeli and the waterfalls are at their fullest.

## Good to know

- Camping is on a 2/3 sharing basis.
- Children under 4 travel free; from 5 years they pay the adult price.
- Food in coastal Karnataka may not be Telugu style; carry snacks if you're particular.
- A booking amount of ₹2,000 holds your seat, with the balance due a week before departure.
