## Why visit Kashmir

Kashmir packs a lot of India's most famous mountain scenery into a few days: Dal Lake and its shikaras and houseboats, the Gulmarg gondola into the snow, Sonmarg's glaciers and the valleys of Pahalgam. Each is a day trip from Srinagar, which makes a 5-day group trip very achievable.

## How HCC's Kashmir trip works

HCC's **[Kashmir trip](/trips/kashmir-from-srinagar/)** starts and ends in **Srinagar**, with airport pickup and drop included. Flights to Srinagar are booked separately.

- **Day 1, Srinagar:** a shikara ride on Dal Lake, the Mughal gardens of Nishat and Shalimar Bagh, and the Tulip or Botanical Garden.
- **Day 2, Gulmarg:** the gondola towards Apharwat peak, snow activities from December to June or horse riding from July to November, and Drung waterfall.
- **Day 3, Sonmarg:** Thajiwas glacier, Baltal and the meadows along the Sindh river.
- **Day 4, Pahalgam:** Chandanwari, the Lidder river, Betaab Valley and Baisaran, then a night on a **Dal Lake houseboat**.
- **Day 5:** shopping in the Old City for shawls, handicrafts and dry fruits, then the airport drop.

## Price and inclusions

From **₹14,999 per person** for 4 nights / 5 days: 3-star hotels (triple or more sharing) and a houseboat night, breakfast and dinner, a team captain and trek leaders, and permits, parking, tolls and driver charges. Groups are 10–15 people. Entry tickets and activities (gondola, skiing, horse riding) are extra.

## Best time to visit

**December to June** for snow and winter activities in Gulmarg; **July to November** for green meadows, clear valleys and horse riding.

## Good to know

- Carry warm layers (a down jacket in winter), waterproof shoes, sunglasses and sunscreen.
- Plans can change with weather or local advisories; the team captain decides on the day.
