## Intro (above the cards)

> Weekend group trips from Hyderabad to the beaches, hills and forests of South India. Most trips leave on a Thursday or Friday evening by overnight train from Kacheguda or Nampally, with travel, stays and sightseeing arranged by HCC.

## How HCC weekend trips work

HCC weekend trips are small group departures that fit into a normal working week. You leave Hyderabad on a Thursday or Friday evening, travel overnight, and spend two or three days at the destination with an HCC trip captain, then travel back overnight.

- **Getting there:** most trips use overnight sleeper-class trains from Kacheguda or Nampally. Some trips offer a 3AC upgrade (Ooty & Coonoor and Isha: +₹1,400; Gokarna: +₹1,700). The Hampi–Gokarna trip runs by sleeper bus.
- **On the ground:** a tempo traveller or similar vehicle for all the sightseeing in the itinerary.
- **Stays:** homestays, hotels or camps on a sharing basis (usually 3–4 people a room). Several trips offer double or triple rooms for an extra charge.
- **Meals:** these vary by trip. Some include camp meals or breakfasts (Gokarna–Dandeli, Munnar, Lonavala); many budget trips do not. Each trip page lists exactly what is included.
- **Booking:** enquire on WhatsApp and the HCC team confirms your seat. Several trips take a booking amount of ₹2,000 per person, with the balance before departure. ⚑ *Confirm this is the standard process for all weekend trips.*

## Popular weekend trips from Hyderabad

### Beaches and the coast
- **[Gokarna, Dandeli & Murudeshwar](/trips/gokarna-dandeli-from-hyderabad/)**: a night camping at Nirvana Beach and a night at a river camp in Dandeli, with Vibhuthi falls, the Murudeshwar temple, Om Beach and kayaking on the Kali river. Leaves Nampally on Friday at 3:45 pm. From ₹5,999.
- **[Goa](/trips/goa-from-hyderabad/)**: North Goa's beaches and forts, a water sports day and Old Goa, staying at a beachside resort. From ₹7,999.
- **[Pondicherry](/trips/pondicherry-from-hyderabad/)**: the French Quarter, Auroville, the promenade and a boat ride through the Pichavaram mangroves. Leaves Nampally at 5 pm. From ₹7,199.

### Hills, tea and coffee country
- **[Ooty & Coonoor](/trips/ooty-coonoor-from-hyderabad/)**: Mysore Palace, Pykara falls and the Nilgiri toy train to Coonoor. Leaves Kacheguda on Friday at 7 pm on the Mysore Express. From ₹7,999.
- **[Coorg & Chikmagalur](/trips/coorg-chikmagalur-from-hyderabad/)**: Abbey Falls, Mandalpatti, a night at a coffee plantation and sunrise on Mullayanagiri, Karnataka's highest peak. Leaves Kacheguda on Friday at 7 pm. From ₹6,999.
- **[Wayanad](/trips/wayanad-from-hyderabad/)**: the Chembra peak trek, the 900 Kandi glass bridge and Banasura Sagar dam. Leaves Kacheguda on Thursday at 7 pm. From ₹7,999.
- **[Araku Valley, Vanajangi & Vizag](/trips/araku-vanajangi-vizag-from-hyderabad/)**: Borra Caves, waterfalls and coffee country, and a pre-dawn walk to watch the sun rise over a sea of clouds at Vanajangi. Leaves Kacheguda on Thursday at 5:30 pm. From ₹6,999.
- **[Munnar](/trips/munnar-from-hyderabad/)**: tea estates, Top Station and Eravikulam National Park, with 3-star stays and breakfast. From ₹8,999.
- **[Lonavala & Matheran](/trips/lonavala-matheran-from-hyderabad/)**: car-free Matheran, Lonavala's viewpoints, a night by Pawna Lake and the Lohagad Fort trek. Leaves on Thursday. From ₹7,999.

### Heritage and spiritual
- **[Hampi, Gokarna & Murudeshwar](/trips/hampi-gokarna-murudeshwar-from-hyderabad/)**: a full day in Hampi's ruins with a coracle ride, then the coast. 3 nights / 4 days by sleeper bus. From ₹8,999.

## How to choose your weekend trip

- **By the kind of weekend you want:** beach and camping (Gokarna, Goa), green hills and viewpoints (Ooty, Coorg, Wayanad, Munnar, Araku), or heritage (Hampi, Pondicherry).
- **By budget:** a 2-night weekend trip starts from ₹5,999 (Gokarna–Dandeli) and goes up to ₹8,999 (Munnar) per person, before any upgrades.
- **By the day you can leave:** Wayanad, Araku and Lonavala leave on **Thursday** evenings; Ooty, Coorg and Gokarna leave on **Friday** evenings.
- **By when you need to be back:** check the last day on each trip page. Coorg–Chikmagalur reaches Hyderabad around 5:30 am and Araku around 7 am; Gokarna–Dandeli arrives between 10 and 11 am. ⚑ *Confirm return days and times.*
- **By season:** Coorg, Wayanad and the Sahyadri treks are at their greenest in the monsoon; the beaches and Hampi are best from October to February.

## Before you book

- These are budget group trips: comfortable and well organised, not luxury tours.
- Carry a government photo ID; it is needed on the train.
- Sightseeing order can change with weather or local restrictions; the trip captain decides on the day.
- If a departure doesn't reach the minimum group size, HCC may merge it with another batch or move you to the next date.

## Frequently asked questions

**What are the best weekend trips from Hyderabad?**
HCC's most booked weekend trips are Gokarna–Dandeli–Murudeshwar, Ooty & Coonoor, Coorg & Chikmagalur, Wayanad and Araku Valley. For a beach weekend choose Gokarna or Goa; for hills and viewpoints choose Ooty, Coorg, Wayanad or Araku. ⚑ *Confirm this "most booked" order (it follows the popularity order you gave).*

**How do HCC weekend trips travel from Hyderabad?**
Most go by overnight sleeper train from Kacheguda or Nampally station, with a vehicle for local sightseeing at the destination. The Hampi–Gokarna trip uses a sleeper bus, and some trips offer a 3AC upgrade.

**How much does a weekend trip from Hyderabad cost?**
HCC's 2-night weekend trips cost ₹5,999 to ₹8,999 per person, depending on the destination. One-night camping trips near Hyderabad start from ₹1,199. Each trip page shows what the price includes.

**Which day do the trips leave?**
Wayanad, Araku and Lonavala trips leave on Thursday evenings. Ooty, Coorg and Gokarna trips leave on Friday evenings. Exact dates are shown on each trip page.

**Can I join a group trip alone?**
Yes. Solo travellers join HCC's group departures and travel with the group and an HCC trip captain from the start.

**Is food included?**
It depends on the trip. Camp-based trips such as Gokarna–Dandeli include some meals, and Munnar includes breakfast; many budget trips don't include food. Check the "What's included" section on each trip page.
