## Data notes found while checking (not changed)

- **What "end date" means differs by route.** Group A routes use the day the group is back (Hyderabad: Thursday batch → Monday morning; Bangalore Coorg: Friday batch → Sunday). Group B routes use the last trip day, before the overnight journey home: for example Araku ends on Sunday in the data although the group is back on Monday at 7 am. The draft keeps each route's 2026 rule; HCC may want one rule before 2027 goes live.
- **Overlapping batches.** Each Thu/Fri (and Bangalore Fri/Sat) route has two batches on the road at the same time every week, as in 2026.
- **Applying the draft:** 5 routes already have an entry in `tools/departure-schedules.json` (extend its `to` date); 7 routes would need a new entry. Their 2026 dates were created outside the generator, so a new entry covering only 2027 would delete their remaining 2026 files: give it a `from` date in 2026 that covers them. Never add a second entry for the same trip and city.
